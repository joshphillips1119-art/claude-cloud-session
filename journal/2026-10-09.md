## 6. Notes

- **Regime:** a relief rally after the 10-08 liquidation flush. BTC rose 1.1%, from 81.75k to 82.64k, on a 2.4% range, about the 7d median. Bar volatility was the lowest of the week at 7.9 bps against a 7d median of 10.6. Lag-1 autocorrelation went back to slightly negative (-0.05), yet VR(6) was 1.15, so single bars mildly reversed while multi-bar drift continued. Taker-buy ratio was high (0.556) and 52.3% of bars closed up. Activity peaked at 11-14h UTC, the EU afternoon and US pre-open.
- **Catalysts (reported):** the rebound was attributed to Trump saying the US would not strike Iran before the Nov 3 midterms, with oil falling back. It came after about $1.2B of liquidations on the Thursday-evening drop to about 80.4k, and $729M of spot-ETF outflows across 10-07 and 10-08. Commentators called the bounce fragile, with $81k as support and $82-82.5k as resistance. Sources: [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/09/bitcoin-rebounds-to-usd82-000-as-trump-rules-out-iran-strikes), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/bitcoin-price-rebounds-above-82k-082116450.html), [Cointribune](https://www.cointribune.com/en/bitcoin-rebounds-above-82k-after-1-19b-liquidation-wave), [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/bitcoin-price-recovers-as-trump-eases-iran-fears/).
- **What worked:** nothing clearly. Thresholded, the ensemble hit 46.5% on 101 calls, below every baseline. Unthresholded it hit 52.1%, which only matches always-up. The faded 6-bar momentum and short_horizon_reversal were the best members (52.4%). taker_flow (faded) lost (45.8%), consistent with persistent buy-side flow on a grind higher. Gross edge was about 0 bps against 10 bps round-trip costs, and cost/move was 1.8, the worst of the week, because the moves were so small.
- **Pattern worth the user's attention (no gate changed):** on all 4 scored days, the calls that cleared the 0.15 abstention threshold did *worse* than the ensemble's calls on every bar. Thresholded: 172/370 = 46.5%. Every bar: 591/1150 = 51.4%. That suggests confidence is currently anti-calibrated: large |scores| come from several reversion members agreeing after big moves, and those are the bars that kept trending. The planned fix is the `selective_cost_gate` / `online_logistic_stacker` framework items, which calibrate confidence out-of-sample. I am not loosening or removing the threshold myself.
- **Changes:** `ema_trend` moved one step to a faded 5/21 crossover (train wz +3.73, val +0.32). Today's new method, `regime_conditioned_autocorr`, was implemented and rejected. Its best config was volume-tercile bins, sign mode, a 20-day window and n0=1500. It scored train wz +1.81 (51.4%, n=6275), below the 3.0 bar, and val wz +2.06 (52.4%, n=1434). Adding it lifted ensemble validation wz from +0.47 to +0.72, so it is the most promising rejected candidate so far, and it will be re-trialled in 7 days. History is only 35 days, so the 60/90-day windows from the spec are not yet testable.
- **Mechanism fix:** `python -m scalper trial --adopt` did not save rejected candidates, so the 7-day re-trial cooldown never applied to them. That is why `intraday_tsmom_session_end` was re-trialled today, one day after its first trial. It now saves the state whether or not the candidate was adopted.

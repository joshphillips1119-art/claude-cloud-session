## 6. Notes

- **Regime:** a −1.34% day (75,868 → 74,851) with a 3.66% range against a 2.19% seven-day median, so the range was wide for the move. Lag-1 autocorrelation was +0.03 and the variance ratio 1.29, hence the "trending" label, but the efficiency ratio was only 0.09: the day ground around and then broke. Almost all of the activity sat in 21–23h UTC (20+ bps per bar against about 5 bps elsewhere), a Sunday-evening leg down into the Asia open. It was the one day of the last 25 on which **persistence (54.6%) beat anti-persistence (45.4%)**. A web search for a dated catalyst found nothing for 8 Nov 2026; the only hits were hourly prediction-market pages pricing BTC at 75.6–76.0k during US hours, consistent with the drop coming after 21:00 UTC.
- **What worked / failed:** the ensemble called 116 bars at threshold 0.25 and hit 54.3% (CI 45–63%), 52.4% on every bar, but gross was −1.37 bps/call: right more often, wrong on the bigger bars. `ema_trend` (held with `_sign` −1, i.e. faded) was best at 57.3%, and `short_horizon_reversal` worst at 46.2%, which fits a day where the last bar's direction persisted. `shock_reversal` went 3 of 8.
- **Changes:** none. Every re-tune candidate missed the training margin or did worse on validation; the threshold stayed at 0.25. `kyle_impact_residual` had its 7-day re-trial and was rejected again (train wz +2.45 against the 3.0 bar, validation +0.26).
- **New methods tested today (two, because the routine had missed 24 days):**
  - `compression_breakout` (backlog #9, TTM squeeze / NR4-NR7 / inside bar): **rejected**. Best plateau was a *faded* NR7 breakout (k 7, buffer 0.25 ATR, decay 3 bars, `_sign` −1): train wz +1.31 (50.9% of 3,490 calls), below the 3.0 bar; validation +1.54 (53.6% of 686); ensemble validation wz +1.61 → +1.70. The continuation hypothesis failed outright, exactly as `trend_state_momentum` did on 14 Oct: on 5-minute BTC the first close out of a compression box is more often faded than followed, and that is already what the reversal family does.
  - `trend_day_fade_gate` (backlog #16c, flagged in the 14 Oct notes): **rejected as a strategy**. The best config was again the fade (`_sign` −1): train wz +1.56 (53.0% of 1,414 calls, 20% coverage), validation −0.36 (49.2% of 331), ensemble +1.61 → +1.55. Following the session trend once the day looked directional was negative on training. The gate's intended use, dampening the fade family's scores by (1 − gate) inside the ensemble, is a framework change and has not been tested; it would need a `backtest --fresh` replay.
- **Catch-up summary (15 Oct – 8 Nov, 25 days scored in one run):** thresholded ensemble 52.4% of 2,050 calls (z +2.21, gross −0.09 bps); every bar 51.6% of 7,177 against anti-persistence 51.0% and always-up 49.4%. One parameter change passed the gates: `short_horizon_reversal` K 6 → 3, lam 1.0 → 0.6 on 29 Oct. All eight rejected candidates were re-trialled on their 7-day cycle and all were rejected again. No guardrail trip. Regimes: 13 of 25 days labelled mean-reverting, 6 trending, 6 random-walk-like.

Two caveats before reading that as an edge: anti-persistence sits at 51.4% on the same bars, so the ensemble has matched the simplest baseline rather than beaten it; and gross is +0.11 bps/call against a 10 bps round trip, so nothing is tradable.

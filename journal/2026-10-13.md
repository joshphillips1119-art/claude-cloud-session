## 6. Notes

- **Regime:** a high-vol, choppy rebound: +1.6% (81.85k -> 83.17k), a 3.9% range, and lag-1 autocorrelation of -0.205, the strongest mean reversion in the 7-day window. Variance ratio 0.76. The search found no specific news catalyst for 2026-10-13.
- **What worked / failed:** this was the ideal day for fades, yet the ensemble only scored 48.8% of 207 calls (CI 42.1-55.6%), against 53.8% for the anti-persistence baseline. The fade strategies were near 50% (vwap 53.0%, short_horizon_reversal 51.2%, rsi and bollinger 49.5%). They fade multi-bar deviations, while the day's reversal was at lag 1. close_location (-1.10 wz) and donchian_breakout (34.3% of 35) were the worst.
- **Changes:** close_location was flipped to a 3-bar fade (n 1 -> 3, _sign -1) after passing walk-forward (val wz -1.58 -> +0.75). The abstention threshold rose 0.15 -> 0.40 (tuned z +4.69 vs +4.05), so the ensemble will call fewer bars, but only higher-conviction ones.
- **New method tested:** `seasonal_slot_prior` (calendar-slot return prior: time-of-day, clock hour, quarter-hour class). It was rejected: the best config was a faded clock-hour prior, train wz +1.00 against a bar of 3.0. About 40 days of history is far too little to measure 1-3 bps slot effects. It stays registered and is re-trialled weekly.
- **Track record:** 8 scored days, 49.0% of 1160 thresholded calls, log10 K = -0.82. There is no evidence of edge yet, and the evidence is not near "worse than coin" either (that line is -1.30). Net of 10 bps costs every variant loses ~10 bps/call, because the average 5-minute move (7-8 bps) is smaller than the round-trip cost.

Lag-1 autocorrelation turned positive (+0.10, against a 7d median of -0.07) and VR(6) was 1.26, so moves tended to continue, unlike the mean-reverting week before. Volume concentrated in 15-17h UTC, the US morning and midday.
- **Catalysts (reported):** a liquidation wave of roughly $1B+ as BTC broke $81k, spot-ETF outflows, a stronger dollar and higher yields, Brent above $105 on Middle East tension, and FOMC minutes read as hawkish. A partial recovery was attributed to Trump ruling out Iran strikes. The big bars mostly reversed (only 2/8 continued). That fits liquidation cascades that overshoot and then snap back, laid over a trending day. I could not tie the 15h UTC volatility peak to a specific print. - **What worked:** the ensemble's first positive day: 53.4% on 103 calls, and 54.7% unthresholded on all 287 bars, beating every baseline. The 95% interval still includes 50%. Mean-reversion to session VWAP and the faded 6-bar momentum led on wz. Donchian breakout lost (39% on 28 calls) because its breakouts were the liquidation overshoots that reversed. Gross edge was about +0.7 bps per call against a 10 bps round-trip cost, so the system is still deeply net-negative at taker fees (cost/move 1.11).
- **Changes:** no parameter moved. Several best regions (ema_trend fast-fade, close_location 6-bar fade) failed the sign-consistency or validation checks. Today's new method, `intraday_tsmom_session_end`, was rejected: its best configuration had train wz +0.05, far below the bar of 3.0. Its validation figure of 63% on 60 calls is the small-sample luck the gate exists to filter.

That is arguably correct, since a rare signal needs more days of evidence. I'm leaving it for the user rather than loosening the gate.

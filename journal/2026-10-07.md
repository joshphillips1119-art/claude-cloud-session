## 6. Notes

- **Regime.** BTC fell 2.6% (85.5k -> 83.3k) on a slightly wider-than-usual range (3.40% vs 2.46% 7-day median), but 5m bar volatility was normal (10.6 bps). The day was unusual in having **positive** lag-1 autocorrelation (+0.047 vs a 7-day median of -0.074) and a variance ratio above 1, so it was a mild grinding trend rather than the usual choppy, mean-reverting tape. The most active hours were the Asia open (01-02h UTC) and the US pre-open/open (13-14h). A web search found only a minor early dip below 86k ([Pluang](https://pluang.com/en/news-feed/bitcoin-turun-di-bawah-86000-harga-mencapai-85878)) and no clear single catalyst, so I am not attributing the move to news.
- **What worked / failed.** The reversion family (taker_flow faded, rsi, bollinger) was the best at 52-54%, but none was significant on one day. Trend-followers (momentum, ema_trend, autocorr_adaptive) and close_location lost, even though the day trended: next-bar continuation on a 2.6% down day is still only a few bps per bar, below bar noise. The ensemble scored 47.3% on 55 gated calls (CI 34.7-60.2%), indistinguishable from a coin flip and below the anti-persistence baseline (52.8%).
- **Cost reality.** Round-trip cost was 1.45x the mean absolute 5m move, so every strategy, including the best baseline, was net negative (about -10 bps per call). Even a real 52-53% hit rate cannot pay 10 bps taker fees at this horizon. Any live use needs maker fills or a much higher abstention threshold aimed at the largest predicted moves.
- **What changed.** `momentum` flipped to a 6-bar **fade** (n=6, sign -1; train wz +3.81, val +0.38), which moves the system further towards mean reversion. `short_horizon_reversal` passed its gate on the re-trial (train +3.51, val +0.56, ensemble val +0.34 -> +0.36) and is now enabled at a small starting weight (0.058). Yesterday's manual trial had rejected it on val wz +0.04, so this is a marginal pass. Watch whether it holds.
- **New method tested today: `noise_area_breakout`** (Zarattini-Aziz-Barbon noise band + VWAP trailing stop). **Rejected.** The tuner's best configuration was the *inverted* breakout, which agrees with the pattern above: at 5m, BTC breakouts beyond the intraday noise band revert slightly more often than they continue. The published edge is session-level PnL from holding trends, which next-bar sign accuracy does not capture. The code stays registered and is re-trialled every 7 days.
- **Track record.** The track record shows 70/166 (42.2%, z -2.02) over the 2 scored days, and log10 K = -0.51, which is not conclusive either way. The guardrail (5 days, z <= -2) has not tripped yet only because it needs 5 days. If the next 3 days continue at this rate, it will trip. I left all gates unchanged.

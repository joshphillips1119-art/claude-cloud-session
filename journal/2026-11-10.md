## 6. Notes

- **Regime:** a CPI day. The whole day's damage came from a few bars: lag-1 autocorrelation flipped to +0.071 (7-day median -0.041), variance ratio(6) 1.06, and the eight largest bars continued 6 times. Yet the sign baselines still leaned reversal (persistence 49.3%, anti-persistence 50.7%), so the autocorrelation was carried by the handful of large post-print bars while the ordinary bars kept reversing. Activity was concentrated in 14-17h UTC (15h averaged 26.8 bps per bar, twice the day's mean), i.e. the 13:30 UTC release and the US session that followed. Costs equalled the mean move (1.01).
- **Catalysts (web, dated 10 Nov 2026):** US October CPI printed hot, 0.4% m/m against a 0.3% forecast per most outlets (core 0.3%; one outlet reported 0.3% headline, so the figure is not uniform), and coverage tied the drop to rising odds of a December Fed hike, with some outlets citing bets on a half-point move. BTC fell from about 75,300 to below 74,000 within the hour and printed roughly 72,750-72,900 later; one report counted about $200M of liquidations in the five minutes after the release, another about $55.6M over four hours. Analysts flagged 73,000 as support with 69-70k below it.

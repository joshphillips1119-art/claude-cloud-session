## 6. Notes

- **Regime:** this was the quietest day in the dataset. It was a Saturday, and it followed the 10-08 flush and the 10-09 relief rally. BTC rose 0.43%, from 82.64k to 82.99k, on a 0.76% range, about a third of the 7-day median. Bar volatility was 3.4 bps against a 7-day median of 8.6. Bars mildly mean-reverted: lag-1 autocorrelation was -0.06, VR(6) 0.85 and the efficiency ratio 0.07. Activity peaked at 14h and 22h UTC, but even those hours were only around 4 bps.
- **Catalysts:** a web search found no dated coverage of BTC on 10-10. There was no reported catalyst, and no ETF-flow, funding or open-interest note for that day or for Friday 10-09. I am not attributing anything. Reading it as a thin weekend session is my own inference, not something a source states.
- **What worked:** this was the best live day so far. Thresholded, the ensemble hit 98/165 = 59.4% (CI 51.8-66.6%). On every bar it hit 56.1%, against 51.4% for the best baseline (anti-persistence). The members that led were reversion members (rsi, bollinger, faded momentum, short_horizon_reversal) plus the faded ema_trend, which is what a low-vol, fade-friendly tape rewards. donchian_breakout lost (7/22), as you would expect on a range day. Gross edge was still only +0.4 bps/call. Moves were tiny (cost/move 4.4), so every strategy was net negative after a 10 bps round trip. **Directional accuracy is improving, but it is not tradeable as a taker on days like this.**
- **Calibration update on yesterday's flag:** on 10-09 I noted that thresholded calls had underperformed every-bar calls on all 4 scored days. Today reversed that: thresholded 59.4% vs every-bar 56.1%. Pooled over 5 days, thresholded is 270/535 = 50.5% (log10 K -0.10) and every-bar is 751/1435 = 52.3% (log10 K +0.67, K ≈ 4.6, which is not yet the K ≥ 20 needed to confirm an edge). The every-bar stream is therefore still the stronger evidence. The case for calibrating confidence out-of-sample (`selective_cost_gate` / `online_logistic_stacker`) stands. No gate was changed.
- **Changes:** none. No re-tune cleared the margin, the threshold stayed at 0.15, and the guardrail did not trip (5-day z +0.22).
- **New method today: `large_move_reversal`** (drift-burst fade, backlog item 5) was implemented, passed the causality tests, and was **rejected**. The best config faded 3-bar bursts of at least 2.5 seasonal sigma on at least 1.5x slot volume, with a 3-bar decay. Training: 199/378 = 52.6%, wz +0.65, far below the 3.0 bar. Validation: 31/58 = 53.4%, wz +0.66, gross -2.3 bps/call. The reversal direction did win over continuation (sign +1), but triggers cover about 5% of bars and the effect is too weak at a 5-minute horizon. That matches the literature, where most drift-burst reversal plays out over hours. It will be re-trialled in 7 days. Next in the backlog is `kyle_impact_residual`.

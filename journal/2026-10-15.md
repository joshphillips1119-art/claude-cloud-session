## 6. Notes

- **Regime:** a high-vol grind higher. Price went +0.69% (81.68k -> 82.25k) with a 3.55% range. 5m volatility was 16.0 bps against a 9.6 bps 7-day median, about 65% above normal and the most volatile day in the scored window. The largest burst came at 02h UTC (26.9 bps, Asia session), and a second wave ran through the US pre-open/open at 12-14h. Lag-1 autocorrelation was only -0.038, and on the sign of returns persistence (53.5%) beat anti-persistence (46.5%). That is the first day in a while where fading the last bar was clearly wrong. The efficiency ratio was 0.017, so the day was two-way chop around a slow up-drift, not a trend. A quick web search found no dated catalyst I could verify for 2026-10-15, so none is claimed.
- **What worked / failed:** at threshold 0.40 the ensemble made 33 calls (11.5% coverage) and hit 51.5% (CI 35-68%), net -8.4 bps/call. Unthresholded it hit 51.0% of 288, beating anti-persistence (46.5%) but trailing always-up (54.5%) and persistence (53.5%). Faded ema_trend (+1.69 wz), bollinger_reversion and rsi_reversion led. donchian_breakout (33% of 27) was worst again, and close_location (45.1%) suffered from the persistence flip. Round-trip cost is 0.96x the mean 5m move, so even on a high-vol day nothing cleared fees. The only net-positive line, shock_reversal, came from 8 calls.
- **Changes:** no params passed the gates, the abstention threshold stayed at 0.40, and Hedge weights moved a little further toward rsi/bollinger reversion.
- **New method tested:** `compression_breakout` (TTM squeeze / NR-k / NR-k inside-bar box breakouts). It was rejected. The best config was a *faded* NR7 breakout (buf 0.25 x ATR, decay 6 bars): train wz +2.59 (51.8%, n=4766) missed the 3.0 bar, with validation 52.0% (wz +0.97, n=873). It added a little to ensemble validation (+0.67 -> +0.76), but that does not override the training gate. This is the third breakout/continuation idea where only the fade fits (after noise_area_breakout and trend_state_momentum). The 5m BTC tape keeps rewarding mean reversion of breakouts, not continuation.
- **Case for the user (no gate changed):** this restates 10-14's note with one more day of evidence. The thresholded ensemble stands at 49.1% of 1212 calls (log10 K -0.80), and every-bar at 50.9% of 2865 (log10 K -0.11), against anti-persistence at 51.75% over 10 days. Today anti-persistence was the *worst* baseline. Adding it as a fixed Hedge expert would therefore not be free: it would have to earn its weight online. A `backtest --fresh` comparison is still the right way to decide.

## 6. Notes

- **Context for this entry.** Written on 16 Nov: the routine's 14 Nov and 15 Nov runs did not publish anything to `claude/scalper-live`, so this run scored 13, 14 and 15 Nov in sequence from the 12 Nov state. Each day was still scored with the parameters that were live before it (v39 here), so the figures remain out-of-sample.
- **Regime.** The second leg of the slide: -4.40% close to close, range 5.84% (2.3x the 7-day median), 5-minute volatility 17.1 bps against a 13.0 bps median, and no bar-level structure at all (lag-1 autocorrelation -0.008, variance ratio(6) 0.92). Unlike 12 Nov the selling was an Asia-hours event: 02:00-05:00 UTC ran -98, -185, -56 and -64 bps, the 03:00 hour alone printed 4,263 BTC (3.5x the hourly average) and the low of 67,568 came at 05:55. The 06:00 hour bounced +136, Europe recovered another ~+100, and the US session was two-sided: the day's two biggest bars were both *up* (13:30 +99 bps, 15:45 +84 bps) while the 14:00 hour fell -79. The evening leaked lower again (21:00 -75). Only 2 of the 8 largest bars continued.
- **Catalysts (press, low-profile aggregators; not verified).** Coverage points to a seventh straight session of US spot-ETF outflows (about $1.91B over 5-13 Nov on one count, about $1.07B for the 9-13 Nov week on Farside's numbers), rate-hike expectations after firm jobs and inflation data, a technology-stock selloff and over $1.2B of long liquidations in 24 hours. Sources: https://cryptolinks.com/news/bitcoin-etf-outflows-seven-sessions-november-2026 , https://coinpaper.com/38802/bitcoin-et-fs-bleed-for-sixth-straight-day-as-btc-falls-nearly-5 , https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/bitcoin-falls-over-12-amid-etf-outflows-and-rate-hike-fears .
- **What worked and why.** Almost nothing. The thresholded ensemble went 48.9% on 135 calls (gross -2.59 bps) against 50.7% for anti-persistence and 49.3% for persistence: on a day with zero autocorrelation there is no bar-to-bar structure to harvest, and the ensemble's concentration in the fade family (rsi 48.3%, bollinger 48.6%) plus the faded trend pair (ema_trend 47.6%, momentum 47.9%) all landed below 50%. The exceptions were taker_flow at 54.5% (its `_sign` is -1, so it was fading one-sided aggressive flow, which paid in the Asia flush and the US whipsaws) and shock_reversal at 5 of 7. The worst day since 5 Nov, but inside every strategy's noise band.
- **Changes.** No parameter change cleared the gates; the tuner again asked for shorter lookbacks everywhere (RSI 14 -> 5, Bollinger 20 -> 10, EMA fast 5 -> 3) and validation refused every one. The 7-day re-trials of intraday_tsmom_session_end (train wz -0.07) and regime_conditioned_autocorr (+1.70, val +0.26) were rejected again. Thresholded log10 K slipped 0.98 -> 0.88.

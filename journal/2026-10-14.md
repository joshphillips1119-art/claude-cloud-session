## 6. Notes

- **Regime:** a high-vol drift down: -1.8% (83.17k -> 81.68k), a 3.3% range, and 5m volatility 12.7 bps against a 9.6 bps 7-day median, about 30% above normal. Lag-1 autocorrelation was -0.037, close to a random walk and far milder than 10-13's -0.205. The efficiency ratio was 0.08, so the drop was ground out rather than trended. Activity was concentrated in 12-14h UTC (US pre-open and open). The search found no specific news catalyst for 2026-10-14.
- **What worked / failed:** after the threshold rose to 0.40, the ensemble called only 19 bars (6.6% coverage) and hit 52.6%. The CI is 31.7-72.7%, so this says nothing either way, and net was -7.5 bps/call. Unthresholded, it hit 51.4% of 288 bars, against 52.1% for anti-persistence and 50.7% for always-up. Every always-on strategy landed in 48.6-52.1%. rsi_reversion was best (+1.06 wz). donchian_breakout (42.9% of 28) and shock_reversal (1/4) were the worst again.
- **Changes:** none. Every re-tune candidate missed the training margin or did worse on validation. Weights drifted toward taker_flow, rsi_reversion and bollinger_reversion. The abstention threshold stayed at 0.40.
- **New method tested:** `trend_state_momentum` (SuperTrend / Heikin-Ashi trend state and flip, with an optional completed-1h confirmation). It was rejected. The best config was a *faded* SuperTrend state (ATR 20 x 2.0, `_sign -1`): train wz +4.46 (52.5%, n=7170) cleared the 3.0 bar, but validation was flat (wz +0.05, 49.7%, n=1427), and adding it lowered ensemble validation wz from 1.48 to 1.25. The continuation hypothesis failed outright: the only part that fit training was fading the trend state, which duplicates the existing reversal family. It stays registered and is re-trialled weekly.
- **Case for the user (no gate changed):** after 9 days, the thresholded ensemble has 49.0% of 1179 calls (log10 K -0.81), while the plain anti-persistence baseline has 52.3% of 2562 bars. At threshold 0.40, coverage dropped to 6.6% (19 calls/day), which is too few to learn from or score quickly. Two options are worth considering. (a) Add the anti-persistence sign as an expert in the Hedge pool, so the ensemble can at least match its best baseline. (b) Build the backlog's `selective_cost_gate` (ACI coverage control) instead of the current point-estimate threshold tuner. Both change the mechanism, so they should be judged by a `backtest --fresh` replay before adoption.

## 6. Notes

- **Regime.** A down day that was trend at the daily scale and mean-reverting at the bar scale: -3.45% close to close, range 4.37% (1.7x the 7-day median), lag-1 autocorrelation -0.074, variance ratio(6) 0.72, and only 3 of the 8 largest bars continued. The selling was concentrated in the US cash session: the 11:00-14:00 UTC hours ran -69, -7, -56 and -119 bps, with the 14:00 hour alone printing 2,833 BTC of volume (about 7x the overnight hourly average) and the day's biggest bar at 14:40 UTC (-81 bps). Asia and Europe drifted lower on thin volume; 15:00-17:00 UTC retraced about +36 bps, then the evening leaked to the lows. 
- **Catalysts (press, not verified against order flow).** Coverage of the slide points to Tuesday's hotter-than-expected CPI print lifting December rate-hike odds, long-dated Treasury yields rising (10-year quoted near 5.13%), a firmer dollar, and continued spot-ETF outflows: about $95M net on Tuesday, IBIT's eighth straight session of withdrawals, and roughly $1.6B for November so far. The 13 Nov coverage describes the follow-through below $70K (and ~$1.6B of liquidations), which belongs to tomorrow's journal.
- **What worked and why.** The fade family led again (bollinger_reversion 54.2%, short_horizon_reversal 53.1% with the best net at -8.4 bps). The *faded* momentum and ema_trend (both carry `_sign` -1) also scored 53.8% / 54.2%: on a day where big bars reversed 5 of 8 times, every strategy that bets against the last move is rewarded. donchian_breakout, flipped to a fade on 11 Nov, went 55.3% on its 38 calls. vwap_reversion (46.5%) was the laggard: a one-way US session pulled price 2-3 sigma below the session VWAP and kept it there, so fading the distance to VWAP lost repeatedly while fading the *last bar* worked. That is the known failure mode of a VWAP fade on a trend day, and it is exactly what the (rejected) trend_day_fade_gate was meant to catch. shock_reversal went 2 of 7, all inside the 13:00-15:00 UTC leg where shocks did continue.
- **Ensemble.** 55.9% on 118 calls at threshold 0.25 (coverage 41%) against 50.7% for the best baseline; gross +0.65 bps per call, so the 10 bp round trip still eats it whole. Hedge moved weight toward bollinger_reversion and momentum(fade) and away from vwap_reversion and autocorr_adaptive. No parameter change cleared the gates; the tuner wanted shorter lookbacks almost everywhere (RSI 14 -> 2, Bollinger 20 -> 10, momentum 6 -> 3), which fits a fast mean-reverting week, but every one of those was worse on the 5 validation days, so one-step rule and validation held. Thresholded log10 K moved 0.78 -> 0.98, still short of the 1.30 "edge confirmed" line; the every-bar series (1.98) stays confirmed but, as before, matches the anti-persistence baseline (51.4%) within noise.

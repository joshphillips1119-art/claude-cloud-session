# Methodology notes (research synthesis, 2026-10-07)

Recommendations from four research passes (academic, microstructure, practitioner,
anti-overfitting) merged into one list. What the framework already implements is
marked; the rest are candidate improvements to the *learning machinery*. Judge
them with `python -m scalper backtest` (out-of-sample replay) before adopting.

Implemented now: 30-day walk-forward window with 5 validation days and an
embargo; plateau selection; luck hurdle E[max Z_N]; one-grid-step moves;
sign-flip day-consistency; fixed-share Hedge with a null expert, equal-weight
blend (0.5) and caps (0.35 per method, 0.5 per family); a new-strategy gate at
t >= 3 (Harvey-Liu-Zhu); an abstention threshold with a coverage floor; a z-based guardrail.

Not yet: CPCV/PBO, Diebold-Mariano tests, HAC errors, Platt calibration, an
anytime-valid martingale/CUSUM rollback, online FDR (LORD++) for trials,
ACI coverage control, and a shadow-mode challenger (online logistic stacker).

## 1. Common notation (CN), causality and warm-up conventions used by every formula

Bar t has open time ts_t and values O,H,L,C,V, plus optional TB (taker_buy_base_volume) and NT (number_of_trades). Compute only on closed bars (Binance websocket kline.x == true); bar t's own OHLCV/TB/NT may be used. Label: y_{t+1} = sign(C_{t+1} - C_t). Ties are excluded from scoring and the tie rate is logged; tick size makes ties common: ES 0.25 pt is about 0.4 bps and SPY $0.01 about 0.15 bps. r_t = ln(C_t/C_{t-1}). slot(t): crypto = UTC minute-of-day/5 (0..287); SPY/ES-RTH = minutes since 09:30 America/New_York /5 (0..77). Seasonal vol factor (Andersen-Bollerslev style), recomputed once a day from completed days only: m_k = median |r| in slot k over the last 20 days; S_k = clip(m_k/median_j m_j, 0.2, 5); S_k = 1 until 10 days exist. Base vol: v_t = (1-a)*v_{t-1} + a*(r_t/S_slot(t))^2, with a = 1 - 0.5^(1/288) (1/78 for RTH equities), initialized from the first 288 bars; sigma_t = sqrt(v_t), floored at 0.5*tick/C_t. Standardized return: rt_t = clip(r_t/(S_slot(t)*sigma_{t-1}), -10, 10). Taker imbalance i_t = 2*TB_t/V_t - 1 (0 if V_t = 0); this is the exact aggressor side, so no classification is needed for crypto. medV_k = median V in slot k over the last 20 days; vs_t = V_t/medV_slot(t). Parkinson pk_t = ln(H/L)^2/(4 ln 2). ATR_n = Wilder RMA of true range. Session VWAP uses TP = (H+L+C)/3. Session anchors are shared utilities, DST-aware through the tz database and never fixed UTC offsets: equity_rth 09:30-16:00 America/New_York; 00:00UTC; us_open_ny 09:30 America/New_York; eu_open 08:00 Europe/London. Every rolling statistic, quantile, OLS fit and calibrator uses windows ending at t; any regression that scores bar t is fit on [t-W, t-1]. Higher-timeframe inputs use completed HTF bars only. Missing inputs or an incomplete warm-up give score_t = 0 with covered_t = false, never NaN and never forward-filled. Clip every score to [-1,1] last. Equities: score 0 on the last RTH bar (t+1 is the gap bar), and exclude the first bar of each session from vol estimators. Implement multi-bar setups as causal state machines updated once per bar (score = direction*confidence*exp(-bars_since_trigger/decay)). Add a unit test that shifts inputs forward by one bar: any edge that survives the shift is leakage.

Sources: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0927-5398(97)00004-2, https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12142, https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/rest-api/market-data-endpoints, https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000481029-strategy-produces-unrealistic-results-on-non-standard-chart-types-heikin-ashi-renko-etc/

## 2. Seasonal normalization of volume, trade count and volatility

Remove time-of-day effects before z-scoring volume, trade count or range volatility, using per-slot medians and MAD over the trailing 7-28 days (default 20). Crypto shows about 80% excess activity in the first 10 seconds of each hour, 30-50% at :15/:30/:45, smaller bursts every 5 minutes, and session effects at the Asia, EU and US opens. Without seasonal adjustment, 'volume surprise' and 'trade intensity' mostly measure the clock. For ES/SPY use session-relative slots and exclude open and close auction bars. Keep pure clock effects in seasonal_slot_prior, separate from the microstructure signals. Range estimators are for normalization and gating only, never direction. For 24/7 crypto default to Parkinson: open prices are noisy with no auction, and in a 5-crypto study Parkinson beat Garman-Klass while Rogers-Satchell fit worst. For ES/SPY handle gaps with Yang-Zhang or drop gap bars.

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09426, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10015199/, https://osuva.uwasa.fi/handle/11111/20509

## 3. Realistic effect sizes and statistical power

Assume 5-minute direction is 50.0-53% predictable. The best recent out-of-sample crypto evidence (15-minute reversal) is AUC about 0.527-0.531 with about 1.3 bps gross per trade. SPY intraday momentum (30-minute R2 about 1.6-2%) is about 51.5% per 5-minute bar. Treat claims of 54-65% minute-level ML accuracy as optimistic or selection-biased. Hit-rate SE = 0.5/sqrt(N): 1 day (288 bars) 2.9%, 30 days 0.54%, 180 days 0.22%. Bars needed versus 50% (one-sided alpha 0.05, power 0.8): 51% needs 15,454; 52% needs 3,862; 53% needs 1,715; 55% needs 616. Two-sided: 19,620 / 4,903 / 2,178 / 783. Wilson 95% half-width at 52%: plus or minus 3.1 pp at n=1,000, 1.55 pp at 4,000, 0.98 pp at 10,000; the lower bound first clears 50% at about n=2,450. Calendar time: crypto 288 bars/day (52% takes about 13.4 days, 51% about 54 days); ES RTH 78 bars/day (52% about 50 days); divide by coverage for gated methods. A calibrated 52% edge improves log-loss by only about 0.0008 nats per bar. Any skill-estimation window or discount must have effective N at or above the required sample: half-life >= 10 days for crypto at 52%. Daily re-tuning on a few days of data fits noise.

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888, https://github.com/jjakimoto/research-issues/issues/1451, https://alphaarchitect.com/attention-prop-traders-the-first-half-hour-of-trading-predicts-the-last-half-hour/, https://projecteuclid.org/euclid.ss/1009213286, https://repository.bilkent.edu.tr/bitstream/11693/77044/1/Prediction_of_cryptocurrency_returns_using_machine_learning.pdf

## 4. Evaluation metrics (per method and for the ensemble)

Primary metric: calibrated log-loss (and Brier score) against the p=0.5 baseline; both are proper scoring rules, so use them for all weighting and promotion decisions. Also report: (a) hit rate on non-tie, nonzero-score bars with a Wilson interval and the Pesaran-Timmermann test, which corrects for constant-sign forecasters in drifting markets; (b) AUC; (c) IC = corr(score_t, r_{t+1}); (d) return-weighted accuracy sum(sign(score)*r_{t+1})/sum|r_{t+1}| and the gross edge mean(score*r_{t+1}) in bps, since trend and breakout methods (noise area, ORB at about a 24% trade hit rate) can be profitable with under 50% hits; (e) cost-adjusted edge mean(score_t*r_{t+1}) - c*mean|score_t - score_{t-1}| at c = 2, 5 and 10 bps, and gross bps per trade in the top 5-10% of |score|; (f) coverage for gated or sparse methods, weighting sparse signals by conditional performance times coverage rather than averaging zeros as votes. Checks: a permutation null that shuffles scores against targets within day blocks; a bounce check that re-scores against sign(C_{t+2} - C_t) or skips one bar; and a frozen holdout block (for example a 60-day block reserved each quarter) never touched by daily re-tuning.

Sources: https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.diagnostic.pesaran_timmermann.html, https://www.cxoadvisory.com/technical-trading/day-trading-with-an-opening-range-breakout-strategy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888

## 5. Purged and embargoed walk-forward for the daily re-tune

Outer loop: true walk-forward on a rolling window. Crypto default 60 days (17,280 bars), grid {30, 60, 90, 180}, never below 30. ES/SPY default 120 RTH sessions, grid {60, 120, 250}. Each day, train on [d-W, d-1], score day d, and only then use day d for selection. Inner hyperparameter search: combinatorial purged CV, 6 contiguous groups with 2 test groups (15 splits, 5 paths). Purge training bars whose label interval overlaps a test bar. Embargo max(1% of T, longest feature lookback, 12 bars), and at least 1 day for session-based methods (noise area, tsmom, ORB). Fit every normalizer and calibrator inside the training fold. Use HAC errors (Newey-West lag about 12 bars) or a stationary block bootstrap with 1-day blocks. Keep each method's daily grid at 50 configurations or fewer and search coordinate-wise: tune core parameters first (for example K, lam, mode), then gates. Re-tune at 00:00 UTC for crypto and before the open for equities.

Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purged_cross-validation, https://skfolio.org/generated/skfolio.model_selection.CombinatorialPurgedCV.html, https://www.econometricsociety.org/publications/econometrica/2006/11/01/tests-conditional-predictive-ability, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7227998

## 6. Overfitting controls on each re-tune: PBO, deflated hurdle, plateau selection, partial moves, sign stability

(a) Keep a permanent trial registry of every configuration and retune ever evaluated; N is cumulative and never resets. (b) Luck hurdle for hit rate: h*(N,n) = 0.5 + 0.5*E[maxZ_N]/sqrt(n), with E[maxZ_N] = (1-g)*InvPhi(1-1/N) + g*InvPhi(1-1/(N*e)) and g = 0.5772. E[maxZ_N] is 1.58 for N=10, 2.53 for N=100 and 3.26 for N=1,000; the best of 100 noise configurations shows 52.8% over 2,000 bars by luck. Promote only if the hit rate exceeds h* and the Wilson lower bound exceeds 0.5. For PnL, require Deflated Sharpe >= 0.95 using per-bar Sharpe, with N = effective trials from correlation clustering (ONC, or clusters at rho > 0.7). (c) PBO via CSCV on a T x N matrix of hourly (12-bar) out-of-sample log-loss gains, S = 16 blocks (12,870 splits; S = 10 gives 252). Accept new parameters only if PBO <= 0.25. If the out-of-sample-on-in-sample slope is <= 0, freeze parameters and update only the ensemble weights. (d) Pick a plateau, not a peak: score each configuration by the mean over itself and its plus-or-minus-1 grid neighbours, and require neighbours to be positive too. (e) Move partially: theta_new = theta_old + 0.25*(theta* - theta_old); integer lookbacks move at most one grid step per day. Keep theta_old unless theta* beats it with a Diebold-Mariano HAC t >= 2, or by more than 2 standard errors versus the literature default. (f) Free sign parameters (d, mode, gamma, side) need the same sign in at least 70% of folds. Sparse methods need at least 100 triggers in the window. (g) Expect BTC to be the hardest market: Hudson & Urquhart found in-sample profitability across about 15,000 rules but none out of sample for BTC.

Sources: https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551, https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253, https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/quantf/v19y2019i9p1555-1565.html, https://www.risk.net/journal-of-computational-finance/2471206/the-probability-of-backtest-overfitting, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10479-019-03357-1

## 7. Ensemble structure, family caps and calibration

Use full-information exponential weights (hedge_fixed_share_ensemble) as the champion combiner and online_logistic_stacker as the challenger. Do not use bandits (EXP3, Thompson) for weighting; reserve them for scarce live-capital slots, with discount 0.9998 per bar if used. Always include a null expert (p=0.5) and keep an equal-weight ensemble running in shadow. Families: reversal (short_horizon_reversal, regime_conditioned_autocorr, large_move_reversal, short_oscillator_reversion, vwap_band_reversion, three_bar_reversal); trend/breakout (noise_area_breakout, intraday_tsmom_session_end, trend_state_momentum, ema_ribbon_pullback, opening_range_breakout, compression_breakout); flow (kyle_impact_residual, vpin_gated_flow, trade_size_intensity); seasonal. Cap any single method at 0.35 and any family at 0.5; send excess to the null expert. Blend learned and equal weights: w = (1-lambda)*w_learned + lambda/K with lambda = 0.5 (grid 0.25/0.5/0.75), per the forecast-combination puzzle. A new method stays at equal weight until it has 4,000 shadow bars. Keep the pure 'fade last candle' baseline (K=1, sign) as a permanent expert so conditioning gains are measurable. Use VPIN, Kyle lambda, Amihud, squeeze state and ATR percentile as gates or meta-features, keeping a gate only if its marginal AUC over the ungated signal is robustly positive. Gating direction: amplify trend and breakout signals on high-volume, high-volatility sessions and after squeeze releases; favour reversion in quiet hours. Calibrate each method's scores with daily Platt scaling on the trailing 20-30 days (purged), clip ensemble p to [0.40, 0.60], and shrink p' = 0.5 + s*(p - 0.5) with s = 0.5 when the shadow sample is under 4,000 bars. A healthy calibration slope is 0.5-1.5; a slope <= 0 triggers rollback. Switch methods off (a_k = 0) when their trailing skill is negative instead of flipping them; only regime_conditioned_autocorr may output either sign by construction.

Sources: https://www.ee.columbia.edu/~sfchang/course/spr/papers/freund95decisiontheoretic-adaboost.pdf, https://mlanthology.org/mlj/1998/herbster1998mlj-tracking, https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04216, https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.00475, https://research.google.com/pubs/archive/41159.pdf, https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437, https://repec.econ.au.dk/repec/creates/rp/13/rp13_42.pdf, https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v10y2013i1p27-33.html

## 8. Multiple testing when candidates are added continuously

Treat each new candidate method or configuration as one hypothesis in an online FDR procedure. Get its p-value from a one-sided HAC Diebold-Mariano test (Newey-West lag 12, or a 1-day block bootstrap) comparing ensemble-plus-candidate against the incumbent on log-loss, over a forward shadow period of at least 4,000 bars (about 14 crypto days, about 50 ES RTH days) not used to design it. Accept with LORD++ at q = 0.10, or alpha-investing with initial wealth 0.05 and payout 0.05. Simpler fallback: t >= 3.0 (the Harvey-Liu-Zhu hurdle). For roster-versus-benchmark comparisons use Hansen's SPA or Romano-Wolf StepM. Use the Model Confidence Set to identify methods statistically indistinguishable from the best. Count every candidate, passed or failed, in the deflated-Sharpe N.

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.06197, https://bioc.r-universe.dev/onlineFDR/doc/theory.Rmd, https://www.nber.org/papers/w20592, https://econ-papers.upf.edu/papers/712.pdf, https://arch.readthedocs.io/en/latest/multiple-comparison/multiple-comparison-reference.html

## 9. Anytime-valid monitoring and champion/challenger rollback

Re-running a fixed-sample test daily inflates false positives: with true p = 0.5, a daily one-sided 5% z-test falsely flags an edge in 23.6% of runs within 30 days and 30.4% within 90 days. Use a betting martingale K_t = prod(1 + lambda*(2x_i - 1)) over predicted non-tie bars, with lambda = 0.04 for p1 = 0.52 (or adaptive in [0, 0.1] from past data only). Declare an edge when K_t >= 20; Ville's inequality bounds false alarms at 5%. Expect about 3,750 bars to detect a true 52%. Two-sided SPRT for 0.50 versus 0.52 (alpha 0.05, beta 0.2): bounds +2.77 and -1.56, +0.0392 per hit and -0.0408 per miss, about 2,380 bars under H1 and 1,680 under H0. Shadow-run the champion, previous champion, equal weight and null expert, and keep the last 5 good versions. Roll back if any of these fire: (i) DM t < -2.0 versus the previous champion over the trailing 864 bars (10 days for ES RTH); (ii) a Bernoulli CUSUM S_t = max(0, S_{t-1} + miss_t - 0.495) crosses h = 25 (simulated mean time to alarm: about 9,750 bars at a true 52%, 1,990 at 50%, 590 at 47%); (iii) the Wilson upper bound over the last 2,000 predicted bars falls below 0.50; (iv) net drawdown exceeds 3x the backtest's 95th-percentile daily drawdown; (v) the calibration slope over 3,000 bars is <= 0. Ladder: previous champion, then equal weights, then abstain on every bar until the martingale crosses 20 again. ADWIN or Page-Hinkley on per-bar log-loss are drop-in alternatives. Log every rollback as a trial.

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240, https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09686, https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01948, https://menelaus.readthedocs.io/en/latest/menelaus.change_detection.html

## 10. Costs, bid-ask bounce and tradability

Break-even hit rate for a 1-bar round trip with move size independent of correctness: p_be = 0.5 + c/(2*E|r|). Measure E|r| causally per instrument. BTC 5-minute E|r| is about 8-14 bps: at c = 10 bps p_be is about 0.86-1.0 (unreachable); at c = 2 bps it is about 0.57-0.62. Binance spot VIP0 is 0.10% per side (20 bps round trip, 15 with the BNB discount); USD-M futures taker is about 5 bps per side. ES is about 0.5-1 bp round trip against an E|r| of 4-8 bps (p_be about 0.55-0.60). SPY at about 0.2-0.5 bp gives p_be about 0.52-0.55, the only setting where a 52-53% edge could plausibly pay. Every gross edge in this set (about 0.5-1.5 bps per bar) is below taker costs, so no method is a standalone taker signal. Options: (1) maker-only execution or quote skewing on the predicted side, accepting adverse selection; (2) trade only when selective_cost_gate passes (top 5-10% of |score| with expected move above cost); (3) hold through consecutive same-sign predictions and pay only on flips, preferring persistent, low-turnover signals (noise area, trend state); (4) use forecasts to time entries and exits for slower strategies. Charge fees, 2x half-spread and at least 1 tick or 1 bp per side of slippage in every walk-forward, and report net PnL daily. Apply a large haircut to published intraday Sharpes: the 5-minute ORB was gross-only and a five-index replication netted about 0; the QQQ VWAP 'holy grail' Sharpe 2.1 came out much lower in replications. Last-trade closes carry bid-ask bounce that inflates measured reversal; Grant-Wolf-Yu's S&P futures reversals lost most of their significance after a spread proxy.

Sources: https://cryptopotato.com/binance-fees/, https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000150665, https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2606.00060, https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jbfina/v29y2005i5p1311-1327.html, https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/776235, https://www.quantconnect.com/forum/discussion/16706, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888

## 11. Selective prediction with coverage constraints

Predict (for trading) only when |p - 0.5| >= tau_t AND (2p - 1)*E_t|r| >= c_rt + buffer, where E_t|r| is a causal EWMA of |r|/S (24-bar half-life) times S of the next slot. Target coverage c* = 20% (allowed band 10-40%). Set tau daily as the (1 - c*) quantile of |p - 0.5| on validation, then adapt online: tau_{t+1} = tau_t + 0.005*(g_t - c*), clamped to [0, 0.1]. Accept a threshold only if the 95% Clopper-Pearson upper bound on selective error over at least 1,000 selected validation bars is below 0.5 (Geifman-El-Yaniv, delta = 0.05). The coverage target never overrides the cost condition. If coverage stays below 5% for 5 days, flag the instrument as not tradable at its current costs. At 20% coverage, confirming 52% takes about 19,300 bars; below 10% coverage, daily statistics are meaningless. Always log the ungated score for accuracy research.

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.11277, https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08500, https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170

## 12. Non-stationarity and decay

Expect effects to decay or flip. McLean-Pontiff found anomaly returns 26% lower out of sample and 58% lower after publication. Gao et al.'s first-to-last half-hour effect reportedly weakened after 2013. BTC intraday seasonals are time-varying (Baur et al.). Turn-of-the-candle appeared only in mid-2020. BTC's short-interval Hurst exponent rose from about 0.42 (2019-20) to about 0.49 (2021-22). Order-flow predictability is regime-unstable: in Schmalz's BTC/USDT OFI study the out-of-sample sign flipped twice as the sample grew from 7 to 9 to 17 days. Track rolling 30/90/180-day statistics per method, use forgetting factors with half-lives of 10-60 days (never under 10 for crypto), let weights decay toward zero when the rolling cost-adjusted IC turns negative, and run a CUSUM change detector on each method's per-bar log-loss contribution. Do not hard-code literature parameters as permanent; shrink toward them instead.

Sources: https://ivey.uwo.ca/media/3775549/pontiff.pdf, https://research-repository.uwa.edu.au/en/publications/bitcoin-time-of-day-day-of-week-and-month-of-year-effects-in-retu/, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10015199/, https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11930v2, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7227998, https://eranraviv.com/market-intraday-momentum

## 13. Instrument portability (crypto versus ES/SPY) and session anchors

Crypto: 288 slots a day, 7 days a week, no gaps. Session-based methods (noise area, tsmom, ORB, VWAP) take the anchor as a tunable parameter from {00:00 UTC, eu_open 08:00 London, us_open_ny 09:30 New York}. The US open is the most defensible, because BTC volatility and liquidity peak at major stock-market opens (Eross et al.) and BTC intraday momentum is strongest with volume-defined sessions (Shen et al.). Rolling 288-bar windows are the anchor-free fallback. Calendar flags that need only timestamps are allowed: Binance funding times (00:00/08:00/16:00 UTC) as clock anchors only, since funding rates are not available, and Deribit Friday 08:00 UTC expiry as a candidate event flag; gamma exposure needs options data and is excluded. SPY: 78 RTH slots; handle the gap-containing first bar separately; overnight returns enter tsmom and the noise area through P0/Cprev = previous close. ES: trades about 23 hours, but use cash-session (09:30-16:00 ET) slots for session methods, because the documented effects are tied to the cash session. Instrument expectations: short_horizon_reversal about null for SPY/ES and strongest for crypto (especially alts, where costs are also higher); tsmom and noise area strongest for SPY/ES; seasonal qh_class and trade_size_intensity are crypto-only; kyle_impact_residual and vpin_gated_flow fall back to CLV/BVC proxies and are weak without taker data. number_of_trades is venue-specific (Binance aggregates fills).

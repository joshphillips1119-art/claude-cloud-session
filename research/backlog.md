## short_horizon_reversal

**Short-horizon candle reversal with taker-flow gate (fade the last candle or candles)**. Status: rejected (2026-10-07 trial, window to 2026-10-06): train wz +3.50 (52.1%, n=7172) cleared bar 3.0, val wz +0.04 (50.1%, n=1425), ensemble val wz -0.685 -> -0.702.

- **Hypothesis:** On liquid crypto pairs, the sign of the last 5-15 minute candle tends to reverse. Liquidity providers who absorb aggressive taker flow are paid through a partial reversal, so the effect is concentrated after bars whose move agreed with the taker imbalance and carried high flow intensity. The information sits mostly in the sign of recent returns rather than their size, fades within hours, and is essentially absent in US equities and ETFs. Merged from the academic 'short_horizon_sign_reversal' and the microstructure 'flow_driven_reversal'.
- **Formula:** Uses the common notation (CN, methodology item 1). Input series: x_t = rt_t when ret_def='cc'; when ret_def='oc', x_t = clip(ln(C_t/O_t)/(S_slot(t)*sigma_{t-1}), -10, 10). Kernel: u_t = sum_{k=0..K-1} lam^k*phi(x_{t-k}) / sum_{k=0..K-1} lam^k. phi(x) = sign(x) with phi(0)=0 when mode='sign'; phi(x) = clip(x,-2,2)/2 when mode='mag'. So |u_t| <= 1. Flow gate, used only when taker_buy_base_volume exists (otherwise G_t = 1): fd_t = 1 if sign(i_t) == sign(r_t) and |i_t| >= theta, else 0; intensity_t = |i_t|*min(vs_t, 3), where vs_t = V_t/medV_slot(t); G_t = w0 + (1-w0)*fd_t*min(1, intensity_t/I_ref), so G_t is in [0,1]. Score: score_t = -clip(kappa*u_t*G_t, -1, 1). Warm-up: mode='sign' needs K+1 bars. mode='mag' and ret_def='oc' need sigma (>= 288 bars) and S (S=1 until 10 completed days). Before that, score_t = 0. Keep the baseline (K=1, mode='sign', kappa=1, w0=1), which reduces to score_t = -sign(r_t), as a permanent reference expert. Do not use the BVC proxy for i_t here: BVC imbalance is a monotone function of r_t, so the gate adds nothing.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"ret_def": ["cc", "oc"], "K": [1, 3, 6, 12], "lam": [0.6, 1.0], "mode": ["sign", "mag"], "kappa": [1.0, 2.0], "w0": [0.0, 0.5, 1.0], "theta": [0.0, 0.1], "I_ref": [0.2, 0.4]}`
- **Evidence:** Kitron & Wengrowicz (arXiv 2608.21888, Aug 2026; existence and abstract confirmed via RePEc listing) ran a strictly out-of-sample walk-forward on 15-minute candles. 90% of 183 Binance USDT pairs showed significant directional reversal, against 2.7% of 187 US stocks and ETFs. Lag-1 autocorrelation is about 0 on the majors, but betting against the previous candle captures most of the effect. The class-mean AUC gap versus equities is +0.031 (95% CI +0.027 to +0.035), or +0.011 under the most conservative accounting. About 70% of bars are 'flow-driven' (the move agrees with 2*TB/V-1), and the reversal concentrates in those bars and grows with flow intensity. The effect decays to no skill within hours. A third-party critique (GitHub issue, confirmed by fetch) reports AUC 0.527 for one lag and 0.531 for a 12-lag kernel, about 1.3 bps gross per trade, no depth, latency or adverse-selection costs, volume ranking done after the sample ended (survivor tilt), and that the authors disclose running an automated trading system on variants of the model. Supporting evidence: De Nicola (Ledger 2021) finds significant negative first-order BTC autocorrelation at 1-6 hours, with larger moves reversing more. arXiv 2003.13517 finds significant 5-minute autocorrelation on major crypto markets, negative at small scales and partly bid-ask bounce. Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka (2010) attribute reversal at horizons under an hour to temporary liquidity imbalances plus bounce.
- **Caveats:** The headline evidence is at 15 minutes, not 5. Expect a 5-minute AUC of about 0.50-0.53 on BTC/ETH (hit rate about 51-53%). The gross edge of about 1.3 bps is below any 2-10 bps taker round trip, so this is an ensemble feature, a maker-side quote skew, or a high-|score| filter, not a standalone taker strategy. Part of the measured last-trade reversal is bid-ask bounce that cannot be captured by crossing the spread; check robustness with the skip-one-bar target. Expect about zero for SPY/ES and let the ensemble weight go to 0 there. The sign can weaken or flip in strong trend regimes; regime_conditioned_autocorr models that, and this method should not be flipped by the ensemble. The method is highly collinear with regime_conditioned_autocorr, large_move_reversal and short_oscillator_reversion, so treat them as one family with a capped combined weight. Full texts could not be fetched (egress blocked).
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888, https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2608.21888.html, https://github.com/jjakimoto/research-issues/issues/1451, https://ledger.pitt.edu/ojs/ledger/article/view/213, https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13517, https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11930v2, https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1005.3535, https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/rest-api/market-data-endpoints
- **Trial log:** 2026-10-07: implemented as `scalper/strategies/short_horizon_reversal.py` (grid K x lam x mode x w0 = 24, ret_def=cc, kappa=1, theta=0, I_ref=0.3 fixed; invertible=False). Best config {K: 6, lam: 1.0, mode: mag, w0: 0.5}. Train 52.1% (95% CI 50.9-53.2%), wz +3.50, gross +0.15 bps; validation 50.1% (47.5-52.7%), wz +0.04, gross -0.04 bps. Adding it lowered ensemble validation wz -0.685 -> -0.702, so rejected. Edge on training is far below the 10 bps round trip, as the caveats predicted. Re-trialled automatically every 7 days.

## noise_area_breakout

**Noise-area intraday momentum with VWAP trailing stop (Zarattini-Aziz-Barbon)**. Status: rejected 2026-10-08 (implemented in `scalper/strategies/noise_area_breakout.py`; re-trialled every 7 days).

- **Hypothesis:** Moves that stay inside a time-of-day-specific noise band around the session open are noise. A close outside the band, confirmed by VWAP, signals a supply/demand imbalance that tends to persist for the rest of the session. VWAP and the band act as a trailing stop. The vwap_only variant reduces to 'long above session VWAP, short below'. Merged from the academic 'noise_area_breakout' and the practitioner 'noise_area_vwap_trend'.
- **Formula:** Uses CN. Session start bar s0 = anchor(t). j = t - s0 is the 0-based slot within the session. O_s = O_{s0}. Cprev = C_{s0-1}: the previous RTH close for equities; for crypto this is the previous bar's close, about O_s, so there is no gap. Noise width: sig_j = mean over the D most recent completed sessions d of |C_{s0_d+j}/O_{s0_d} - 1|. At least 0.7*D sessions must have slot j, otherwise score_t = 0. UB_t = max(O_s, Cprev)*(1 + mult*sig_j); LB_t = min(O_s, Cprev)*(1 - mult*sig_j). VWAP_t is the session VWAP from s0 to t (CN). State pos in {-1,0,+1}, reset to 0 at s0. variant='noise_area', processed in this order each bar. (1) Stops, every bar: if pos=+1 and C_t < (vwap_stop ? max(UB_t, VWAP_t) : UB_t), set pos=0; if pos=-1 and C_t > (vwap_stop ? min(LB_t, VWAP_t) : LB_t), set pos=0. (2) Entries and flips, only on decision bars where (j+1) mod check_every == 0: if C_t > UB_t and C_t > VWAP_t, set pos=+1; else if C_t < LB_t and C_t < VWAP_t, set pos=-1. (3) Excursion: e_t = max(0, (pos=+1 ? C_t - UB_t : LB_t - C_t))/(O_s*sig_j). score_t = pos_t*clip(m_min + g*e_t, 0, 1). variant='vwap_only': for j >= 6, pos_t = sign(C_t - VWAP_t) if |C_t - VWAP_t| > deadzone_atr*ATR14_t, else pos_{t-1}; score_t = pos_t*min(1, |C_t - VWAP_t|/ATR14_t). Equities: score_t = 0 on the last RTH bar, because t+1 is the next session's gap bar. Warm-up: score 0 until D completed sessions exist.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"variant": ["noise_area", "vwap_only"], "anchor": ["equity_rth", "00:00UTC", "us_open_ny"], "D": [10, 14, 20], "mult": [0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5], "check_every": [1, 6], "vwap_stop": [true, false], "m_min": [0.3], "g": [1.0, 3.0], "deadzone_atr": [0.0, 0.25]}`
- **Evidence:** Zarattini, Aziz & Barbon, 'Beat the Market' (SFI WP 24-97; details confirmed via search): on SPY from 2007 to early 2024, a 14-day noise area with multiplier 1, a gap adjustment, half-hourly entry checks and VWAP/band trailing stops returned 1,985% in total net of costs (19.6% a year, Sharpe 1.33). It placed 4th in the Quantpedia Awards 2025. Zarattini & Aziz, 'VWAP: The Holy Grail' (QQQ, 1-minute bars, 2018-2023): Sharpe 2.1 and max drawdown 9.4%, but public QuantConnect replications came out much lower. Concretum's BTC intraday trend ensemble (2018-2025) had a gross Sharpe of about 1.6, against just under 0.8 for vol-targeted buy and hold. Academic support for intraday continuation: Gao et al. (JFE 2018), Li, Sakkas & Urquhart (JFM 2022), and Shen, Urquhart & Wang (Financial Review 2022, BTC).
- **Caveats:** The published results are strategy PnL with positive skew from trailing stops on trend days, not next-bar sign accuracy. The per-bar hit rate may be only about 50-51% even where the strategy pays, so evaluate with return-weighted and cost-adjusted edge, not raw accuracy. It is the most fee-robust method here because positions persist for many bars and turnover is low. Evidence is medium for SPY/ES/QQQ. For crypto, the 00:00 UTC open is conventional rather than structural, so treat it as low confidence and let the anchor be tuned (us_open_ny is the most defensible). Replications are lower than published figures, and the effect may decay after publication. It overlaps with intraday_tsmom_session_end and trend_state_momentum (trend family cap) and is anti-correlated with vwap_band_reversion.
- **Sources:** https://www.sfi.ch/de/publications/n-24-97-beat-the-market-an-effective-intraday-momentum-strategy-for-s-p500-etf-spy, https://ideas.repec.org/p/chf/rpseri/rp2497.html, https://concretumgroup.com/beat-the-market-an-effective-intraday-momentum-strategy-for-sp500-etf-spy/, https://concretumgroup.com/volume-weighted-average-price-vwap-the-holy-grail-for-day-trading-systems/, https://concretumgroup.com/seasonality-in-bitcoin-intraday-trend-trading/, https://www.quantconnect.com/forum/discussion/16706, https://www.quantconnect.com/reports/2dd44a5c1394e0ba3de9c7f4d7dc0716, https://in.tradingview.com/script/CUpWCZhe-Concretum-Bands, https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v129y2018i2p394-414.html, https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finmar/v57y2022ics138641812100001x.html
- **Trial log:** 2026-10-08 (window to 2026-10-07, 48 configs incl. sign flip; grid trimmed to variant x anchor{00:00UTC, us_open_ny} x mult{0.75,1,1.5} x check_every{1,6}, D=14 fixed): rejected. The tuner preferred the *faded* breakout (_sign -1), i.e. on BTC 5m bars a close outside the noise band mean-reverts slightly rather than continuing, consistent with the negative lag-1 autocorrelation seen most days. Train wz +2.06 < bar 3.0; val wz -0.06; adding it lowered ensemble val wz +0.363 -> +0.244. As the caveats predicted, a trend-holding signal scored by next-bar sign does not show its published PnL edge.

## intraday_tsmom_session_end

**End-of-session intraday time-series momentum (first or rest-of-session return predicts the last window)**. Status: idea (code not in repo as of 2026-10-07; earlier "implemented" status was wrong).

- **Hypothesis:** Late in the session, the return so far (or the first half-hour return, measured from the previous close) predicts the final 30-60 minutes. Proposed causes: gamma hedging by short-gamma option and leveraged-ETF dealers, infrequent rebalancing, and late-informed trading. The effect is strongest on volatile, high-volume days. Crypto needs a defined session anchor.
- **Formula:** Uses CN. Session [A, E): 'equity_rth' = 09:30-16:00 America/New_York. This covers SPY and ES cash hours, and for crypto it applies the same NY clock to crypto prices on weekdays only. '00:00UTC' = [00:00, 24:00) UTC (crypto). Active iff bar t+1 opens in [E - L_min, E), i.e. t+1 is one of the last L_min/5 bars; otherwise score_t = 0. P0 = close of the last bar ending at A (equities: the previous session's final RTH bar, so the overnight gap is included). Raw predictor: mode='first': R_t = ln(C at A+F_min / P0). mode='rest': R_t = ln(C_t/P0). mode='gao2' (equity_rth only): R1 = ln(C at A+30m / P0) and R12 = ln(C at E-L_min / C at E-L_min-30m). Normalizer: recompute the same quantity at the same clock time in each of the previous 20 sessions; sR = sqrt(mean of squares), with at least 10 sessions required, otherwise score 0. z_t = R_t/sR, or for gao2 z_t = (R1/sR1 + R12/sR12)/sqrt(2). Vol boost: RV_t = sqrt(sum of r_s^2 over session bars in [A, t], excluding the first bar of the session). hv_t = 1 if RV_t exceeds the median RV at the same slot over the previous 20 sessions, else 0. Score: score_t = tanh(c*z_t)*(1 + delta*hv_t)/(1 + delta).
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"anchor": ["equity_rth", "00:00UTC"], "mode": ["first", "rest", "gao2"], "F_min": [30, 60], "L_min": [30, 60], "c": [0.5, 1.0, 2.0], "delta": [0.0, 1.0]}`
- **Evidence:** Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (JFE 2018), SPY 1993-2013: the first half-hour return (measured from the previous close) predicts the last half-hour. In-sample R2 is about 1.6-2% (4.3% in the financial crisis) and out-of-sample R2 is up to 1.8%, or 2.7% with the 12th half-hour added. The effect is stronger on volatile, high-volume and macro-news days. A 2012-2014 replication found correlation 0.12 and R2 0.014. Baltussen, Da, Lammers & Martens (JFE 2021): across 60+ futures from 1974 to 2020, the rest-of-day return predicts the last 30 minutes, linked to short-gamma hedging. Li, Sakkas & Urquhart (JFM 2022): the effect holds out of sample in 16 developed markets, stronger when liquidity is low and volatility high. Shen, Urquhart & Wang (Financial Review 2022): BTC first half-hour predicts last half-hour when sessions are defined by volume. Wen et al. (NAJEF 2022): both intraday momentum and reversal in crypto.
- **Caveats:** Coverage is only 6-12 bars per session. A 30-minute correlation of about 0.12 spread over six 5-minute bars implies a per-bar correlation of about 0.05, or a hit rate of about 51.5%. The effect reportedly weakened after 2013. Medium confidence for SPY/ES; low for crypto, where the anchor is arbitrary and Shen et al.'s exact volume-based session definition could not be verified (full text blocked). For ES, use the 16:00 ET cash close, not the 17:00 ET Globex break. Because R is identical across the active window in mode 'first', the six active bars are not independent observations.
- **Sources:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v129y2018i2p394-414.html, https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/market-intraday-momentum/, https://alphaarchitect.com/attention-prop-traders-the-first-half-hour-of-trading-predicts-the-last-half-hour/, https://cxoadvisory.com/calendar-effects/first-and-last-half-hours-of-trading-linked, https://www3.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf, https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finmar/v57y2022ics138641812100001x.html, https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/bitcoin-intraday-time-series-momentum/, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4080253, https://eranraviv.com/market-intraday-momentum
- **Trial log:** none yet

## regime_conditioned_autocorr

**Regime-conditioned adaptive autocorrelation (volatility, volume and liquidity regimes; LeBaron, Campbell-Grossman-Wang, Nagel)**. Status: idea (code not in repo as of 2026-10-07; earlier "implemented" status was wrong).

- **Hypothesis:** Whether the next bar continues or reverses the last one depends on the regime. Autocorrelation becomes more negative when expected volatility is high (LeBaron), when volume is liquidity-driven (Campbell-Grossman-Wang), and when liquidity is scarce (Nagel, Amihud). It also differs by session and between weekdays and weekends. Estimating the lag-1 coefficient per regime bin with shrinkage lets the data choose momentum or reversal. Merged from the academic 'regime_conditioned_autocorr', the microstructure 'volume_surprise_reversal' (its CGW/LMSW volume gate becomes the volume-tercile bins) and 'liquidity_regime_reversal' (its vol-ratio and Amihud gates become the vol-ratio and illiquidity bins).
- **Formula:** Uses CN. Regime features at t, from bars <= t. Tercile cutpoints (33rd and 67th percentiles) are recomputed once a day from the trailing W_days. vol: vol_feat='level' uses sigma_t; vol_feat='ratio' uses VR_t = sqrt(mean_{k<12} pk_{t-k}/S_slot(t-k)^2) / sqrt(mean_{k<2016} pk_{t-k}/S_slot(t-k)^2). volume: VS_t = (ln(1+V_t) - med)/(1.4826*MAD), where med and MAD are taken over ln(1+V) at slot(t) in the last 20 completed days. illiq: A_t = mean_{k<12} |r_{t-k}|/(C_{t-k}*max(V_{t-k}, Vfloor)), with Vfloor = 1st percentile of V over W_days. session (crypto, assigned in this order): US if 09:30 <= NY time < 16:00; Asia if 00:00 <= UTC < 07:00; EU if UTC >= 07:00 and NY time < 09:30; otherwise Late. Session (equities): open = first 6 bars, close = last 6 bars, otherwise mid. weekend = Saturday/Sunday UTC (crypto only). bin(t) is the tuple of terciles/flags named by 'bins'. Estimation: refit daily at 00:00 UTC, with optional incremental intraday updates of the sums. Use pairs (s, s+1) with s+1 <= t and s in the trailing W_days, binned with the current cutpoints. x_s = clip(rt_s, -cl, cl), y_{s+1} = clip(rt_{s+1}, -cl, cl). mode='slope': rho_b = sum_{bin(s)=b} x_s*y_{s+1} / sum_{bin(s)=b} x_s^2. mode='sign': rho_b = (n_same - n_opp)/n_b, counting only pairs where both returns are nonzero. Shrinkage: rho'_b = rho_b*n_b/(n_b + n0). Prediction: yhat_t = rho'_{bin(t)}*x_t (slope) or rho'_{bin(t)}*sign(r_t) (sign). Score: score_t = tanh(yhat_t/q_ref). Use a fixed q_ref, not a rolling std of yhat, so that when every rho shrinks to 0 the score is also near 0. Warm-up: score 0 until W_days of history and >= 288 bars of sigma exist.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"W_days": [30, 60, 90], "bins": ["vol3", "volume3", "vol3_x_volume3", "vol3_x_session", "vol3_x_weekend", "illiq3"], "vol_feat": ["level", "ratio"], "mode": ["sign", "slope"], "n0": [500, 2000], "cl": [2.0, 3.0], "q_ref": [0.05, 0.1]}`
- **Evidence:** LeBaron (J. Business 1992): first-order autocorrelation is inversely related to volatility. Campbell, Grossman & Wang (QJE 1993): autocorrelation falls with volume because liquidity-driven volume is reversed. Llorente, Michaely, Saar & Wang (RFS 2002): hedging-driven moves reverse while speculative ones continue, which is why the sign per bin is left to the data. LeBaron, Pontiff & Bogousslavsky ('A Century of Market Reversals', 2024): expected volatility and anticipated volume drive reversals. Nagel (RFS 2012): returns to short-term reversal (liquidity provision) rise sharply with VIX. Kitron & Wengrowicz: crypto 15-minute reversal scales with taker-flow intensity. arXiv 2003.13517: intraday volatility is positively linked to short-term predictability in crypto. Range estimators: in a 5-crypto HAR study Parkinson beat Garman-Klass and Rogers-Satchell fit worst. Realized-Amihud work reports that intraday illiquidity predicts short-term returns.
- **Caveats:** LeBaron, CGW, LMSW and Nagel are daily or weekly equity results; transfer to 5-minute crypto is plausible but not established. Binning splits the sample: with 9 bins and 60 days (17,280 pairs) each bin has about 1,900 pairs, so the standard error of rho is about 0.023, the same size as the effects. Keep n0 large and prefer one- or two-feature bins. This method nests short_horizon_reversal (one bin, sign mode); cap their combined weight or orthogonalize. It is the only method allowed to output either sign by construction. Volatility and illiquidity regimes are also where spreads and slippage are widest, which may consume any gain. Floor V in Amihud to avoid blow-ups on near-zero-volume bars.
- **Sources:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/ucp/jnlbus/v65y1992i2p199-219.html, https://dash.harvard.edu/bitstream/1/3128710/2/campbell_trading.pdf, https://www.johnhcochrane.com/s/llorente_michaely_saar_wang_volume_rfs.pdf, https://www.abfer.org/media/abfer-events-2024/annual-conference/papers-investment/AC24P3015-A-Century-of-Market-Reversals--Resurrecting-Volatility.pdf, https://www.nber.org/papers/w17653, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888, https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13517, https://osuva.uwasa.fi/handle/11111/20509, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.15807
- **Trial log:** none yet

## large_move_reversal

**Volatility-standardized large-move (drift-burst) reversal**. Status: idea (code not in repo as of 2026-10-07; earlier "implemented" status was wrong).

- **Hypothesis:** Extreme, high-volume moves (liquidation cascades, demand for immediacy, overreaction) are partly reversed over the following bars, and more so after negative bursts on heavy volume. Because the trigger is rare and the conditional move is large, the expected reversal in bps can be larger than the unconditional short_horizon_reversal edge, which helps against fees.
- **Formula:** Uses CN. Shock z-score with vol from before the shock window: Z_t = ln(C_t/C_{t-m}) / (sigma_{t-m}*sqrt(sum_{j=0..m-1} S_slot(t-j)^2)). Seasonal volume ratio: v_t = sum_{j<m} V_{t-j} / sum_{j<m} medV_slot(t-j). side_ok = 1 for 'both', 1[Z_t < 0] for 'down_only'. Trigger: trig_t = 1[|Z_t| >= theta]*1[v_t >= v_min]*side_ok. On a trigger, set e = -sign(Z_t)*min(1, 0.25 + (|Z_t| - theta)/theta_w), t0 = t and move0 = |C_t - C_{t-m}|. A new trigger replaces the old event. Score: score_t = e*exp(-(t - t0)/decay_bars) for t0 <= t < t0 + 3*decay_bars; with decay_bars = 0, the score is nonzero only at t = t0. Cancel (score 0 from then on) once the reversal has half-completed: -sign(Z_{t0})*(C_t - C_{t0}) >= 0.5*move0. Otherwise score_t = 0. Warm-up: needs sigma (>= 288 bars) and 20 completed days for medV.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"m": [1, 3, 6], "theta": [2.5, 3.0, 4.0], "theta_w": [1.0, 2.0], "v_min": [0.0, 1.5, 3.0], "side": ["both", "down_only"], "decay_bars": [0, 3, 12]}`
- **Evidence:** Christensen, Oomen & Reno (drift-burst hypothesis): drift bursts occur about weekly across asset classes and are mostly followed by reversion, more strongly after negative bursts on large volume. De Nicola (Ledger 2021): larger BTC moves at 1-4 hours are followed by proportionally stronger reversals. Grant, Wolf & Yu (JBF 2005): highly significant reversals after large opening moves in S&P futures, 1987-2002, that largely lose significance after a bid-ask proxy. Miralles-Quiros & Miralles-Quiros (2022): overreaction after negative hourly BTC shocks, mostly 6-24 hours later. Counter-evidence: Scaillet, Treccani & Trevisan (JFEc 2020) find BTC jumps cluster and cause persistent price changes. A 2026 FRL paper finds BTC intraday reversals around Deribit Friday 08:00 UTC expiries; the gamma-exposure conditioning needs options data and is excluded, and only the timestamp flag could be used.
- **Caveats:** Coverage is about 1-5% of bars, so require at least 100 triggers in the tuning window before trusting its weight. Evidence on the next-5-minute sign is mixed: jumps can continue, cascades can keep going, and most documented reversal plays out over hours. The 'side' and 'v_min' choices double the search space. It is collinear with short_horizon_reversal in mode 'mag' (reversal family cap). Spreads widen in exactly these moments, so slippage is the main cost risk.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08974, https://repec.econ.au.dk/repec/creates/rp/16/rp16_28.pdf, https://ledger.pitt.edu/ojs/ledger/article/view/213, https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jbfina/v29y2005i5p1311-1327.html, https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15140326.2022.2151253, https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.08175, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612326008688

## kyle_impact_residual

**Kyle-lambda impact residual (price overshoot versus unpriced taker flow)**. Status: idea (code not in repo as of 2026-10-07; earlier "implemented" status was wrong).

- **Hypothesis:** Contemporaneous return is roughly linear in signed order flow (Cont-Kukanov-Stoikov). If price moved more than the flow justifies, the move is an overshoot (temporary impact) and should revert. If it moved less (passive absorption, flow not yet priced), the unpriced flow should continue. The score loads positively on lagged signed flow and negatively on lagged return. It is the only flow-continuation mechanism in the set.
- **Formula:** Uses CN. Per-bar flow: flow_src='taker' (requires TB) gives f_s = i_s. flow_src='clv' (OHLC-only fallback for ES/SPY) gives f_s = ((C_s - L_s) - (H_s - C_s))/(H_s - L_s), or 0 if H_s == L_s. Do not use BVC here: it is a deterministic function of r_s, which makes the residual degenerate. Signed volume: sf_s = f_s*vs_s for impact='linear', or sign(f_s)*sqrt(|f_s|*vs_s) for impact='sqrt', where vs_s = V_s/medV_slot(s). Winsorize sf at the 0.5/99.5 percentiles of the fit window. Fit at bar t by OLS over s in [t-W, t-1]: rt_s = a + lam*sf_s + e_s; sd_e = residual std. Require >= 0.8*W valid bars and lam > 0, otherwise score_t = 0. Aggregate over n bars ending at t: R_n = sum_{k<n} rt_{t-k}; SF_n = sum_{k<n} sf_{t-k}; u_t = R_n - (n*a + lam*SF_n). Score: score_t = -tanh(u_t/(c*sd_e*sqrt(n))). A positive score means price under-reacted to buy flow, or over-shot downward.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"W": [288, 864, 2016], "n": [1, 3, 6], "c": [1.0, 2.0], "impact": ["linear", "sqrt"], "flow_src": ["taker", "clv"]}`
- **Evidence:** Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov (JFEc 2014): a linear, stable relation between order-flow imbalance and contemporaneous price change. Silantyev (Digital Finance 2019): on BitMEX XBTUSD, trade-flow imbalance explains contemporaneous moves better than book OFI. Anastasopoulos et al. (J. Financial Markets 2026): crypto order flow alone does not predict returns, but the part of lagged flow orthogonal to lagged returns predicts positively (long-short alpha 0.81% a month, t=5.83, daily/weekly cross-section). Chordia & Subrahmanyam (2004): lagged imbalances relate positively to returns. Kitron & Wengrowicz document the overshoot half (reversal after flow-driven moves) at 15 minutes.
- **Caveats:** The flow-continuation evidence is daily/weekly and cross-sectional; at 5 minutes the reversal leg will probably dominate, which makes it partly collinear with short_horizon_reversal. Kline taker imbalance is trade imbalance, not book OFI (no cancels or limit adds), so much of the CKS explanatory power is lost. Predictive R^2 at 5 minutes is likely well under 1%. The CLV fallback is a crude proxy, so expect near-null results for ES/SPY. Kim & Hansen find baseline imbalance has little predictive association at fine horizons.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402, https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/digfin/v1y2019i1d10.1007_s42521-019-00007-w.html, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5020002, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1386418126000029, https://www.efmaefm.org/0EFMAMEETINGS/EFMA%20ANNUAL%20MEETINGS/2025-Greece/papers/OrderFlowpaper.pdf, https://ideas.repec.org/p/cdl/anderf/qt34k8f3pv.html, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888

## seasonal_slot_prior

**Calendar-slot return prior (time-of-day, day-of-week, and quarter-hour turn-of-the-candle)**. Status: idea (code not in repo as of 2026-10-07; earlier "implemented" status was wrong).

- **Hypothesis:** Recurring institutional flows, rebalancing, session opens and algorithms that act on completed 15-minute and 1-hour candles leave small, recurring calendar patterns in returns. The trailing mean or sign rate of returns sharing the calendar key of bar t+1 is a weak prior. Merged from the academic 'same_slot_periodicity' and 'quarter_hour_turn_prior'; both estimate a trailing conditional mean keyed on calendar time.
- **Formula:** Uses CN. key(t+1) comes from the open time of bar t+1, which is known at t, in timezone tz. 'slot_day' = slot(t+1). 'slot_week' = (weekday, slot(t+1)). 'hour' = clock hour, pooling all 12 bars of that hour. 'qh_class' (crypto only) = H if the minute-of-hour is 0, Q if it is 15, 30 or 45, O otherwise. For SPY/ES, slots are session slots and qh_class is disabled. Sample: X = {rt_s : s <= t, key(s) == key(t+1), s within the last D_days days}. If split_weekend (crypto), use only days of the same type (Mon-Fri vs Sat-Sun) as t+1, going back until D_days matching days are found. Baseline Y = all rt_s from the same days. stat='mean': z = (mean(X) - demean*mean(Y))/(std(X)/sqrt(n_X)). stat='sign' (nonzero returns only): p = (n_up + 20)/(n + 40); p0 = 0.5 if demean=0, else the same statistic computed on Y; z = (p - p0)/sqrt(p0*(1 - p0)/n). base_t = tanh(z/c) if n_X >= 10, else 0. Optional hour-open flow add-on (crypto, taker data, imb_w > 0): I_h = i of the most recent bar at or before t whose open minute is 00; score_t = clip(base_t + imb_w*tanh(3*I_h), -1, 1). Otherwise score_t = base_t.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"key": ["slot_day", "slot_week", "hour", "qh_class"], "tz": ["UTC", "America/New_York"], "D_days": [20, 60, 120], "stat": ["mean", "sign"], "demean": [0, 1], "c": [2.0, 3.0, 5.0], "split_weekend": [false, true], "imb_w": [0.0, 0.3]}`
- **Evidence:** Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka (JF 2010): stock half-hour returns persist at daily multiples for 40+ trading days, not explained by volume, volatility or spreads. BTC: Quantpedia (2015-2022) finds 21:00-23:00 UTC hours significantly positive. NYDIG (2021) reports day-of-week effects. Baur et al. find BTC time-of-day and day-of-week effects are time-varying and inconsistent. Shanaev, Vasenin & Stepanov (2023, 'turn-of-the-candle'): +0.58 bps per minute at minutes 0/15/30/45 since mid-2020, with other minutes negative on average. K33 confirms the minute pattern after Nov 2021. Hansen, Kim & Kimbrough (JFEc 2024) and Kim & Hansen (arXiv 2607.09426, confirmed via search; Binance futures 2021-2024) find volatility and volume bursts at :00, :15, :30, :45 and every 5 minutes. Hour-open order imbalance forecasts 4-12 hour returns, but average gains at the marks are far below Binance fees (press summary).
- **Caveats:** Slot means of 1-3 bps against 5-minute standard deviations of 10-30 bps need years of data to reach t=2, so with D_days=20 the estimate is mostly noise. Keep c large and expect a small ensemble weight. The quarter-hour effect is +0.58 bps in minute 0 of a 5-minute bar diluted by four slightly negative minutes, so the net bias may be about 0. It is crypto-only, post-2020 and possibly arbitraged. Crypto seasonals are documented as time-varying. Use the America/New_York timezone for US-linked effects so DST is handled. The hour-open imbalance evidence is for 4-12 hours, not the next bar.
- **Sources:** https://www.bauer.uh.edu/departments/finance/documents/Heston_Korajczyk_Sadka_paper_UH.pdf, https://quantpedia.com/are-there-seasonal-intraday-or-overnight-anomalies-in-bitcoin/, https://nydig.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/NYDIG-The-Temporalities-of-Bitcoin-Returns.pdf, https://research-repository.uwa.edu.au/en/publications/bitcoin-time-of-day-day-of-week-and-month-of-year-effects-in-retu/, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10015199/, https://k33.com/research/archive/articles/all-minutes-are-not-equal-for-bitcoin-traders, https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12142, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09426, https://cryptoslate.com/the-invisible-opening-bell-inside-cryptos-endless-trading-day/

## trend_state_momentum

**Trend-state momentum (SuperTrend or Heikin-Ashi state and flip)**. Status: idea (code not in repo as of 2026-10-07; earlier "implemented" status was wrong).

- **Hypothesis:** An ATR trailing band (SuperTrend) or smoothed Heikin-Ashi candles define a short-term trend state. Being on the bullish side, or having just flipped to it, predicts a slight continuation on the next real bar. It provides a 24/7, anchor-free continuation counterweight to the reversal family, with low turnover. Merged from the practitioner 'supertrend' and 'heikin_ashi_trend', which are collinear smoothed-momentum signs.
- **Formula:** variant='supertrend': hl2 = (H+L)/2; ATR = Wilder ATR(atr_len). bUB_t = hl2_t + mult*ATR_t; bLB_t = hl2_t - mult*ATR_t. fUB_t = bUB_t if (bUB_t < fUB_{t-1} or C_{t-1} > fUB_{t-1}) else fUB_{t-1}. fLB_t = bLB_t if (bLB_t > fLB_{t-1} or C_{t-1} < fLB_{t-1}) else fLB_{t-1}. Initialize fUB_0 = bUB_0, fLB_0 = bLB_0, dir_0 = +1. dir_t = +1 if C_t > fUB_{t-1}; -1 if C_t < fLB_{t-1}; else dir_{t-1}. ST_t = fLB_t if dir_t = +1, else fUB_t. mode='state': score_t = dir_t*(beta + (1 - beta)*tanh(|C_t - ST_t|/(mult*ATR_t))). mode='flip': score_t = dir_t*exp(-bars_since_flip_t/flip_decay). variant='heikin_ashi': O',H',L',C' = EMA_{pre_smooth} of O,H,L,C (pre_smooth=1 means raw). HAC_t = (O'+H'+L'+C')/4; HAO_t = (HAO_{t-1} + HAC_{t-1})/2 with HAO_0 = (O'_0 + C'_0)/2; HAH_t = max(H'_t, HAO_t, HAC_t); HAL_t = min(L'_t, HAO_t, HAC_t). color_t = sign(HAC_t - HAO_t); streak_t = number of consecutive same-color bars ending at t. strong_t = 1 if (color=+1 and HAO_t - HAL_t <= 0.05*ATR14_t) or (color=-1 and HAH_t - HAO_t <= 0.05*ATR14_t), else 0. mode='state': score_t = color_t*min(1, streak_t/n_sat)*(0.5 + 0.5*strong_t). mode='flip': score_t = color_t*exp(-(streak_t - 1)/n_sat). Optional htf_confirm='1h': compute the same variant's direction on completed 1-hour bars only, i.e. the last 1h bar that closed at or before the close of bar t; if it disagrees, multiply the score by 0.5. Warm-up: score 0 for the first max(3*atr_len, 50) bars. Always label and evaluate on real closes, never on HA prices.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"variant": ["supertrend", "heikin_ashi"], "atr_len": [10, 14, 20], "mult": [2.0, 3.0, 4.0], "mode": ["state", "flip"], "beta": [0.3, 1.0], "flip_decay": [3, 12], "pre_smooth": [1, 3, 5], "n_sat": [2, 3, 5], "htf_confirm": ["none", "1h"]}`
- **Evidence:** Weak, practitioner-grade evidence. SuperTrend defaults ATR 10 x 3 (Seban). LiberatedStockTrader (4,052 trades, 30 DJIA stocks): 42-43% win rate, unprofitable on daily bars. Quantified Strategies (60+ years of S&P 500): it mainly reduces drawdowns. Unverified TradingView BTC 5-minute claims (55% win rate, profit factor 2.2) are likely overfit. Heikin-Ashi: a tiny 40-day 5-minute test with no costs. TradingView warns that strategies filled at HA prices are unrealistic. HA color is roughly a short EMA-momentum sign. Related: Corbet et al. (2019) find moving-average rules significant on high-frequency BTC; Hudson & Urquhart (2021), testing about 15,000 rules with data-snooping controls, find no out-of-sample predictability for Bitcoin.
- **Caveats:** Expect at most a tiny next-bar edge. It is included mainly as a low-turnover, anchor-free continuation input so the ensemble can down-weight reversal in trends. It whipsaws in ranges and is collinear with ema_ribbon_pullback and noise_area_breakout (trend family cap). The higher-timeframe confirmation must use only completed 1h bars, or it repaints and looks ahead. The recursive HA open depends on initialization, so respect the warm-up.

## compression_breakout

**Volatility-compression breakout (TTM squeeze, NR4/NR7, inside bar)**. Status: idea (code not in repo as of 2026-10-07; earlier "implemented" status was wrong).

- **Hypothesis:** Volatility compression (Bollinger Bands inside Keltner Channels, or the narrowest range in k bars, optionally also an inside bar) is followed by expansion (Crabel). The first close outside the compression box gives a short-lived direction. Merged from the practitioner 'squeeze_breakout' and 'nr_inside_breakout', which share the contraction-to-expansion mechanism. Its more reliable use is as a regime gate.
- **Formula:** Setup detection at bar i. setup='squeeze': mid = SMA_n(C), sd = population std_n(C), BBu/BBl = mid +/- 2*sd; KCm = EMA_n(C), KCu/KCl = KCm +/- k_kc*ATR_n (Wilder). sq_i = (BBu_i < KCu_i) AND (BBl_i > KCl_i). The setup is armed while sq has held for >= min_bars consecutive bars; box = [min L, max H] over those squeeze bars. setup='nr': R_i = H_i - L_i; armed at i if R_i <= min(R_{i-k+1..i}); box = [L_i, H_i]. setup='nr_inside': as 'nr', plus H_i <= H_{i-1} and L_i >= L_{i-1}. An armed setup expires setup_window bars after its last qualifying bar. Breakout: the first t after arming with C_t > boxH + buf*ATR14_t gives dir=+1; C_t < boxL - buf*ATR14_t gives dir=-1. Record t_b and mag = min(1, 0.5 + |C_t - edge|/max(boxH - boxL, 1e-12)), where edge is boxH for up and boxL for down. Score: score_t = dir*mag*exp(-(t - t_b)/decay_bars) for t_b <= t < t_b + 3*decay_bars. Stop (score 0 from then on) if C_t closes back inside the box. Otherwise score_t = 0. Also export sq_t, an NR flag, and the bandwidth percentile ((BBu - BBl)/mid ranked over the trailing 2016 bars) as regime features for the ensemble and other gates. Warm-up: 3*n bars.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"setup": ["squeeze", "nr", "nr_inside"], "n": [20, 30], "k_kc": [1.5, 2.0], "min_bars": [3, 6, 12], "k": [4, 7], "setup_window": [3, 6], "buf": [0.0, 0.1, 0.25], "decay_bars": [1, 3, 6]}`
- **Evidence:** Practitioner-only and weak. Backtrex BB squeeze on WTI 4h (2016-2026): +73.7% against +94.6% buy and hold, 36% win rate, profit factor 1.21. Quantified Strategies: the squeeze 'doesn't do particularly well' across assets. Crabel (1990) tabulated NR4/NR7 on daily futures and found contraction followed by expansion. Bulkowski: NR7 upward breakouts fail to break even 46% of the time. arXiv 1812.02527: NR patterns are not tradable after costs without additional rules. There is no 5-minute study. Compression reliably predicts a larger next |return| but not its sign.
- **Caveats:** The direction is close to a coin flip, and by the time C_t clears the box part of the move has already happened. Narrow 5-minute bars cluster in dead hours (Asia night, equity lunch) where follow-through is below fees, so consider gating by seasonal volume. Coverage is sparse; require at least 100 breakouts before trusting its weight. It is most valuable as a gate: amplify momentum and breakout signals after a release and dampen reversion.

## vpin_gated_flow

**VPIN toxicity-gated short-horizon signed flow**. Status: idea (code not in repo as of 2026-10-07; earlier "implemented" status was wrong).

- **Hypothesis:** When VPIN (how one-sided recent volume is) is high, the most recent signed flow is more persistent or more toxic, so it should carry more weight; when VPIN is low, flow is noise. VPIN is a magnitude/regime variable, so it gates signed flow, and the continuation-versus-fade sign is learned.
- **Formula:** Uses CN. Buy volume: Vb_t = TB_t when taker data exist. Otherwise BVC: Vb_t = V_t*F((C_t - C_{t-1})/sd_288(dC)), where sd_288 is the trailing std of close changes over 288 bars and F is the normal CDF or a Student-t CDF with nu=0.25 (ELO 2016). Vs_t = V_t - Vb_t. Bar-time VPIN: VPIN_t = sum_{k<n}|Vb - Vs|_{t-k} / sum_{k<n} V_{t-k}. Short imbalance: OI_t = sum_{k<m}(Vb - Vs)_{t-k} / sum_{k<m} V_{t-k}. Gate: g_t = clip((pctrank_L(VPIN_t) - p0)/(1 - p0), 0, 1), where pctrank is over the trailing L bars ending at t. Score: score_t = mode*g_t*tanh(OI_t/c), with mode = +1 (continuation) or -1 (fade). Score 0 if sum V = 0 or before L bars of history.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"n": [12, 50], "m": [1, 3], "p0": [0.5, 0.8], "L": [2016, 8640], "c": [0.2, 0.4], "mode": [1, -1], "flow_src": ["taker", "bvc"]}`
- **Evidence:** Easley, O'Hara, Yang & Zhang (2024): VPIN and Roll measures for BTC/ETH have own- and cross-market predictive power for price dynamics, mainly volatility, liquidity and higher moments rather than return sign. A Coinbase/Bitstamp study (2015-2018) finds VPIN predicts BTC crash risk. Andersen & Bondarenko: VPIN is mechanically tied to volume and volatility, with no incremental volatility forecasting power after controls, and depends on classification. Chakrabarty et al.: the tick rule and Lee-Ready beat BVC on equities. A practitioner BTC test found about a 1.4x lift and no warning before a large drop.
- **Caveats:** There is no direct evidence that VPIN predicts the next 5-minute sign. Its best use is as a confidence multiplier or meta-feature for other signals; report its marginal AUC over the ungated signal. The free 'mode' sign must be stable in at least 70% of folds. Bar-time VPIN only approximates volume-time VPIN. With BVC, OI is a function of returns, so it is nearly redundant with the reversal family.

## short_oscillator_reversion

**Short-oscillator and close-location reversion (RSI(2), ConnorsRSI, IBS/CLV, Chaikin money flow) with trend filter**. Status: idea.

- **Hypothesis:** Extreme readings of very short oscillators, and closes pinned at the bar's high or low (especially on volume), mark temporary aggressor pressure or overextension that partly reverts on the next bar, preferably in the direction of the longer trend. Merged from the practitioner 'connors_short_reversion' and the microstructure 'clv_money_flow_fade'. IBS = (1+CLV)/2, so both are the same close-location fade.
- **Formula:** RSI_n is Wilder's (RMA of gains and losses, alpha = 1/n). Oscillator X_t: variant 'rsi2' uses RSI_2(C)_t. Variant 'crsi': streak_t = +k after k consecutive higher closes, -k after k lower closes, 0 if unchanged; PR_t = percentage of the last 100 one-bar returns below r_t; X_t = (RSI_3(C)_t + RSI_2(streak)_t + PR_t)/3. Oscillator fade: s_osc = clip((Lo - X_t)/Lo, 0, 1) - clip((X_t - (100 - Lo))/Lo, 0, 1). Close location: IBS_t = (C_t - L_t)/(H_t - L_t), set to 0.5 if (H_t - L_t) < min_range_bps*1e-4*C_t; s_loc = clip((band - IBS_t)/band, 0, 1) - clip((IBS_t - (1 - band))/band, 0, 1). Money flow: CLV_s = 2*IBS_s - 1; CMF_t = sum_{k<n} CLV_{t-k}*V_{t-k} / sum_{k<n} V_{t-k}; s_cmf = -tanh(CMF_t/0.5). raw: s_osc for rsi2/crsi; s_loc for 'ibs'; s_cmf for 'cmf'; clip(0.6*s_osc + 0.4*s_loc, -1, 1) for 'rsi2_ibs'. Trend filter: tr_t = sign(C_t - SMA_trend_len(C)_t); w = 1 if raw == 0 or sign(raw) == tr_t, else ctw. Score: score_t = d*raw*w, with d = +1 (fade, default) or -1 (continuation). d = -1 may receive weight only if it wins in at least 70% of walk-forward folds. Warm-up: max(10*n_rsi, 101, trend_len) bars.

## vwap_band_reversion

**Session-VWAP standard-deviation band reversion**. Status: idea.

- **Hypothesis:** When the close is stretched far from session VWAP in volume-weighted standard deviations and the bar shows rejection, price tends to drift back toward VWAP. This is the counterpart of the VWAP-trend rule in noise_area_breakout, and the ensemble decides which applies in each regime.
- **Formula:** Uses CN anchors. Session start s0 is one of: equity_rth; 'utc0' = 00:00 UTC; 'us_open' = 09:30 America/New_York; 'rolling_288' = t-287. TP_i = (H_i+L_i+C_i)/3. Over i in [s0, t]: VWAP_t = sum V_i*TP_i / sum V_i; sd_t = sqrt(max(sum V_i*TP_i^2 / sum V_i - VWAP_t^2, 0)); sd*_t = max(sd_t, 0.25*ATR14_t). If t - s0 + 1 < min_session_bars, score_t = 0. z_t = (C_t - VWAP_t)/sd*_t. base_t = -sign(z_t)*clip((|z_t| - k_entry)/1.0, 0, 1). If require_rejection and z_t > 0: keep base_t only if H_t >= VWAP_t + k_entry*sd*_t and the upper-wick fraction (H_t - max(O_t, C_t))/(H_t - L_t) >= 0.4; mirror this for z_t < 0 with the lower wick; otherwise base_t = 0. Trend damping: slope_t = (VWAP_t - VWAP_{t-6})/ATR14_t (requires t-6 >= s0, else slope = 0). If sign(slope_t) == sign(z_t), base_t = base_t/(1 + lambda_trend*|slope_t|). score_t = clip(base_t, -1, 1).
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"anchor": ["equity_rth", "utc0", "us_open", "rolling_288"], "k_entry": [1.5, 2.0, 2.5], "lambda_trend": [0.0, 0.5, 1.0], "require_rejection": [false, true], "min_session_bars": [6, 12]}`
- **Evidence:** Practitioner evidence only, mixed to negative. Backtrex VWAP 2-sigma fade on DAX 15-minute bars (2016-2026): -98.6% against +137.5% buy and hold, profit factor 0.64. A TradingView XLE 5-minute script had a 69% win rate yet still lagged buy and hold. Zarattini & Aziz find the opposite rule (VWAP trend) profitable on QQQ. Indirect support comes from negative short-lag autocorrelation in crypto.
- **Caveats:** High win rate with negative skew: it keeps losing on trend days. Coverage is a few percent of bars. The crypto anchor is arbitrary, and a rolling VWAP drops the benchmark rationale. A 1-2 bar reversion of a 2-sigma 5-minute stretch is often below cost. It is anti-correlated with noise_area_breakout, which helps diversification but means the regime weighting must be learned.

**Opening-range breakout at session opens with relative-volume gate**. Status: idea.

- **Hypothesis:** The first 5-60 minutes after a session open set a range. Its direction, or a later close outside it, tends to continue, especially when opening-range volume is high relative to its own history (the single-instrument analogue of 'stocks in play').
- **Formula:** Anchor a: equity_rth 09:30 ET; crypto 'utc0' 00:00 UTC, 'eu_open' 08:00 Europe/London, or 'us_open' 09:30 America/New_York. All anchors are DST-aware. The OR window is bars [a, a+or_bars-1]: ORH = max H, ORL = min L, ORV = sum V. RVOL = ORV/mean(ORV over the previous 14 sessions at the same anchor). Before the OR completes, score_t = 0. variant='direction': at e = a+or_bars-1, dir = sign(C_e - O_a) if |C_e - O_a| > 0.05*ATR14_e, else 0; t0 = e. variant='breakout': the first t in (e, a+48] with C_t > ORH gives dir=+1, or with C_t < ORL gives dir=-1; t0 = t. Stops: 'or_opposite' stops a long if C_t < ORL and a short if C_t > ORH. 'atr_frac' stops a long if C_t < C_t0 - atr_frac*ATRd14, mirrored for shorts, where ATRd14 = mean session true range over the previous 14 sessions. After a stop, dir = 0 for the rest of the session. g = use_rvol ? clip((RVOL - rvol_min)/(2.0 - rvol_min), 0, 1) : 1. score_t = dir*g*exp(-(t - t0)/decay_bars) while active; 0 after a stop, and 0 on the last bar of an equity session.

Holmberg, Lonnbark & Lundstrom (FRL 2013): crude-oil ORB returns significantly above a fair game, varying strongly with volatility state. There is no rigorous crypto study.

## trade_size_intensity

**Trade-size versus trade-count conditioned direction**. Status: idea.

- **Hypothesis:** Binance klines report number_of_trades, so average trade size is V/N. Bars with unusually large average trade size more likely carry informed flow, so the move continues (LMSW). **Three-bar reversal pattern**. Status: idea.

- **Hypothesis:** Two bars in one direction, then a bar that makes the middle bar the swing extreme and closes beyond the prior bar's high or low, signal exhaustion and a short reversal of the prior swing, especially at an N-bar extreme. On t+1 this is a continuation of bar t's direction.

On a pattern: dir = +/-1, t0 = t, mag = min(1, 0.5 + |C_t - ref|/(gamma_atr*ATR14_t)). Score: score_t = dir*mag*exp(-(t - t0)/decay_bars) for t0 <= t < t0 + 3*decay_bars. ## hedge_fixed_share_ensemble

**Fixed-share exponentially weighted forecaster on log-loss with a null expert**. Status: framework: done (fixed-share Hedge + null expert + family caps + equal blend in scalper/ensemble.py).

- **Hypothesis:** Every base method's would-be prediction is observed on every bar, so this is a full-information experts problem. Exponential weights on calibrated log-loss, with eta=1 being a Bayesian mixture, track whichever method is currently best, with regret bounded by ln(K+1) nats. A fixed-share term allows switching across regimes. A null expert (p=0.5) lets the ensemble fall back to no signal. This is the daily-updated ensemble-weighting layer.
- **Formula:** Experts k=1..K are the base scores s_k,t in [-1,1], each at its currently selected parameters. Expert 0 is null, with p_0 = 0.5. Daily calibration at 00:00 UTC, on the trailing 30 days with purging: fit the logistic regression 1{C_{s+1} > C_s} ~ b0 + b1*s_k,s on non-tie bars where s_k,s != 0. a_k = clip(calib_shrink*b1/2, 0, a_max). Set a_k = 0 if the HAC t-stat of b1 is below 1. Methods are switched off, not sign-flipped. Expert probability: p_k,t = 0.5 + 0.5*a_k*s_k,t. Online update at the close of bar t, scoring the predictions made at t-1: skip if C_t == C_{t-1}. Otherwise y = 1{C_t > C_{t-1}}; l_k = -[y*ln p_k,t-1 + (1-y)*ln(1-p_k,t-1)]; v_k = w_k*exp(-eta*l_k); w_k <- (1-alpha_fs)*v_k/sum_j v_j + alpha_fs/(K+1). Optional discounted variant replacing fixed share: L_k <- beta*L_k + l_k, w_k proportional to exp(-eta*L_k), beta = 0.5^(1/(H_days*bars_per_day)). Caps after every update: w_k <= 0.35 for each non-null expert, and the sum per family <= 0.5. Families: reversal (short_horizon_reversal, regime_conditioned_autocorr, large_move_reversal, short_oscillator_reversion, vwap_band_reversion, three_bar_reversal); trend (noise_area_breakout, intraday_tsmom_session_end, trend_state_momentum, ema_ribbon_pullback, opening_range_breakout, compression_breakout); flow (kyle_impact_residual, vpin_gated_flow, trade_size_intensity); seasonal (seasonal_slot_prior). Excess weight moves to the null expert. Initial weights are 1/(K+1). Ensemble: p_t = sum_k w_k*p_k,t. Score: score_t = clip((2*p_t - 1)/a_max, -1, 1).
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"eta": [0.25, 0.5, 1.0], "fixed_share_alpha": [0.0, 0.0001, 0.001], "a_max": [0.1, 0.2], "calib_shrink": [0.5, 1.0], "discount_half_life_days": [null, 10, 20]}`
- **Evidence:** Hedge (Freund & Schapire 1997) has regret at most sqrt((T/2) ln K). On log-loss with eta=1 it is Vovk's aggregating algorithm or a Bayes mixture, with total regret at most ln(K+1): about 3.0 nats for K=20. Fixed share (Herbster & Warmuth 1998) bounds tracking regret against the best sequence of experts with S switches. For scale, a true 52% expert gains about 0.0008 nats per bar, about 6.9 nats per 30 crypto days, so the regret allowance absorbs about half the edge in a month. Bandit methods (EXP3, Thompson sampling) discard counterfactual information here. Forecast-combination literature (Rapach-Strauss-Zhou RFS 2010; the combination puzzle, Wang et al. 2022) supports shrinking toward equal weights.
- **Caveats:** It needs calibrated experts: an over-confident expert loses to the null expert even with a real 52% edge, which is why the Platt-derived a_k and the clip at a_max exist. Regret is relative to the best expert in hindsight, so it cannot tell skill from luck; promotion of new experts must still pass the online-FDR and deflated-hurdle gates. A convex combination ignores correlation, so with eta >= 1 weight piles onto whichever correlated expert got lucky. Prefer eta 0.25-0.5 plus the family caps. It creates no edge: if all experts are coin flips, it converges to the null expert, which is the correct outcome.

## selective_cost_gate

**Confidence- and cost-gated selective prediction with adaptive coverage**. Status: framework: partial (threshold tuner with coverage floor; ACI online coverage + cost condition not yet built).

- **Hypothesis:** Most 5-minute bars have no predictable direction, and fees exceed the expected edge on almost all of them. Abstaining unless the ensemble is confident and its expected move clears round-trip cost raises accuracy on the bars it does predict and is the only route to net profitability. An adaptive threshold holds coverage at a target so statistics remain measurable.
- **Formula:** Inputs: ensemble probability p_t (from hedge_fixed_share_ensemble or online_logistic_stacker), returns r, slots (CN). Expected absolute next move: m_t = EWMA_{half-life = vol_hl bars}(|r_s|/S_slot(s)) at t, times S_slot(t+1). The next slot is known at t. Expected edge: E_t = (2*p_t - 1)*m_t, in log-return units. c_rt = round-trip fee + 2*half-spread + slippage, in log units (10 bps = 0.0010). Gate: g_t = 1 if |p_t - 0.5| >= tau_t AND |E_t| >= c_rt + b, else 0. With hold_rule='same_sign', if g_{t-1}=1 and sign(p_t - 0.5) == sign(p_{t-1} - 0.5), the cost term is waived because the position is held and no new cost is paid, but the tau condition still applies. Score: score_t = g_t*clip((2*p_t - 1)/(2*p_cap - 1), -1, 1). Online coverage control (ACI): tau_{t+1} = clip(tau_t + gamma*(g_t - c_star), 0, 0.1). Daily reset: candidate tau* = the (1 - c_star) quantile of |p - 0.5| over the trailing validation window. Accept it only if, over at least 1,000 validation bars with |p - 0.5| >= tau*, the 95% Clopper-Pearson upper bound on selective error is < 0.5; otherwise keep the previous tau. Never lower c_rt to raise coverage. If realized coverage is below 5% for 5 consecutive days, flag the instrument as 'not tradable at current cost'. Always log the ungated ensemble score separately for accuracy metrics.
- **Research grid (shrink to <= 30 combos):** `{"c_star": [0.1, 0.2, 0.3], "gamma": [0.001, 0.005, 0.01], "b_bps": [0, 1, 2], "vol_hl": [12, 24, 48], "p_cap": [0.55, 0.6], "hold_rule": ["strict", "same_sign"]}`

Geifman & El-Yaniv (2017): selective-risk guarantees via a threshold chosen with a binomial bound. Gibbs & Candes (2021): the ACI update achieves the long-run target frequency under arbitrary distribution shift. Jaquart et al. (2021): 1-60 minute BTC predictability is profitable before costs and negative after. A 2026 hourly BTC-USDT walk-forward (about 70k hours, 27 folds) found naive sign trading fails at 10 bps, and a cost-threshold filter rescued only some configurations.
- **Caveats:** It helps only if accuracy actually rises with |p - 0.5|; check daily with an accuracy-by-decile table. The cost condition will almost never pass on BTC 5-minute bars at Binance VIP0 spot taker fees (about 15-20 bps round trip) or even 10 bps: with E|r| of about 8-14 bps and p about 0.52, E_t is about 0.3-0.6 bps. That is an honest outcome, so report it rather than tuning around it. Lower coverage slows validation: at 20% coverage, confirming 52% takes about 19,300 bars (about 67 crypto days, about 250 ES RTH days).

## online_logistic_stacker

**Online logistic stacker (FTRL-Proximal, shrink-to-prior, forgetting)**. Status: framework: idea (challenger combiner; needs a shadow-mode harness).

- **Hypothesis:** A regularized online logistic regression on the base scores, pulled toward equal weights and slowly forgetting old data, can handle correlated base signals (including negative partial weights) better than a convex expert mixture. It gives slightly better-calibrated probabilities, an expected gain of at most about 0.5-2 percentage points of hit rate. It is deferred only because Hedge plus equal-weight shrinkage covers the first release; use it as the challenger once base-score correlations are observed.
- **Formula:** Features: x_t = [1, s_1,t, ..., s_K,t], with K <= 15. Optionally add 1-2 interactions s_k,t*volz_t, where volz_t is the z-score of the causal EWMA sigma_t against its trailing 30-day distribution. p_t = sigmoid(w . x_t). At the close of bar t, skip ties; otherwise y_t = 1{C_t > C_{t-1}} labels the prediction made at t-1, and g = (p_{t-1} - y_t)*x_{t-1}. FTRL-Proximal is run on u = w - w0, where the prior w0 is 0 for the intercept and 1/K per score; the gradient is unchanged because the loss is evaluated at w = u + w0. Per coordinate i: sig_i = (sqrt(n_i + g_i^2) - sqrt(n_i))/alpha; z_i += g_i - sig_i*u_i; n_i += g_i^2; u_i = 0 if |z_i| <= l1, else u_i = -(z_i - sign(z_i)*l1)/((beta + sqrt(n_i))/alpha + l2). w = clip(u + w0, -2, 2). Forgetting: once a day multiply all n_i and z_i by rho = 0.5^(1/H_days). Score: score_t = clip(c*(2*p_t - 1)/0.2, -1, 1), on the same scale as hedge_fixed_share_ensemble. Daily retune of (alpha, l1, l2, H_days, c) by purged CPCV on the trailing window, moving the live values only part of the way: new = old + 0.25*(chosen - old). Unit test: shifting labels forward by one bar must destroy any edge.

- **Evidence:** FTRL-Proximal with per-coordinate learning rates and elastic-net regularization is the standard streaming logistic regression method (McMahan et al., KDD 2013). Rapach, Strauss & Zhou (RFS 2010): combined forecasts beat the benchmark out of sample where individual predictors fail. The forecast-combination puzzle (Wang et al. 2022 review; arXiv 1505.00475) shows estimated weights often lose to equal weights, hence the shrinkage to the equal-weight prior. Expected gain: a calibrated 52% predictor improves log-loss by only about 0.0008 nats per bar.
- **Caveats:** Collinear scores make individual weights unstable; keep K <= 15, orthogonalize nested pairs (short_horizon_reversal versus regime_conditioned_autocorr), and rely on the l2 pull toward the prior. A half-life under about 10 days (fewer than about 4,000 effective crypto bars) chases noise. A calibration slope <= 0 over the last 3,000 bars means it is anti-predictive and must be rolled back.

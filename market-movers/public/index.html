Which strategies — the playbook

📐 EMA 9/21 Pullback

Trend-following · the bread & butter
When it works
Trending sessions — London/NY overlap for crypto, first 90 min for stocks.
Setup
EMA9 above EMA21 and price above session VWAP (longs; mirror for shorts). Wait for a pullback that tags EMA9 without closing below EMA21.
Entry
Buy stop at the pullback candle's high + 0.1×ATR. No fill in 2–3 candles → cancel.
Stop loss
Below the pullback swing low − 0.3×ATR.
Exit
Half off at 1R, rest at 2R or when a candle closes through EMA21. Move stop to break-even after T1.

🧲 VWAP Snap-back

Mean reversion · fade the stretch
When it works
Chop and lunch hours; after an overextended spike without follow-through.
Setup
Price ≥1.5×ATR away from session VWAP with 5m RSI(14) under 32 (long) or over 68 (short). Skip it if EMAs are steeply stacked against you — don't fade a freight train.
Entry
First candle that reclaims its previous high (long) / breaks its previous low (short).
Stop loss
0.5×ATR beyond the extreme of the move.
Exit
Half at the midpoint back to VWAP, rest at VWAP. Don't hold through VWAP hoping for more.

🌀 Coil Breakout

Range expansion · two-sided
When it works
Quiet coils before session opens and after news digests; low ATR about to expand.
Setup
The last hour (12 × 5m bars) ranges less than ~2×ATR — compression.
Entry
Bracket it: buy stop above the range high + 0.1×ATR, sell stop below the range low − 0.1×ATR. Cancel the other side once one fills.
Stop loss
Range midpoint.
Exit
T1 at 1× the range height, T2 at 2×, or trail behind the 5-bar low/high. Beware fakeouts — prefer the break in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend.
Risk rules (non-negotiable for scalping): risk ≤ 1% of account per trade — size = (account × 0.01) ÷ |entry − stop|. Keep R:R ≥ 1.5, use limit/stop orders (market orders bleed spread on 5m timeframes), and stand aside 5 minutes either side of major news (CPI, FOMC, NFP). Three losses in a row = done for the session. Fees and slippage eat scalpers — count them. This is an analysis tool, not investment advice.
